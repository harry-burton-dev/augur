Folio finds documents by tags, filters and saved views. Each library has its own tags and shares the org's (kept by the leads on the org's floor); leads can let anyone add tags. Documents are tagged as they're uploaded or afterwards. The list filters by tags, file type, who added it and when it changed, looking in every folder below, and sorts by title, newest or recently changed, a page at a time. A filter set can be saved as a view in the library's side rail for everyone there.
