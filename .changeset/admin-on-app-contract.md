Admin is on the app contract (contract 3) and runs in the shell's worker like Deployment. Its places are People, Departments, Organisation, Apps and Activity. Each person, department, app and Activity entry has a page of its own, and every simple setting is changed where it's shown: a name or email saves as you leave the field, a role or department as you pick it. A change that may ask for your password again (deleting someone, two-step for everyone, how long leavers' and Activity's records are kept) opens a question at a path of its own, so signing in again comes back to it. An app's page in Admin opens the same app's page in Deployment for what the server runs for it.
