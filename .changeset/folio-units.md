Artifacts gets its form (PER-171, S1 and S2). A library is a collection, and its folders are subcollections, series and subseries, which can be skipped but never turned round. Every version of a file has a lifecycle (in progress, in review, complete, issued, retired), and every collection and folder one too (open, closed, archived), with each move on the record. The org's leads can rename the levels. Existing libraries move over by themselves: deeper folders become subcollections named for where they were, each series with a schedule gets its own series, and versions out to an audience are issued. The move is one way: the nightly backup from before the upgrade is the way back.
