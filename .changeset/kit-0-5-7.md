Serves kit 0.5.7 (Quill 0.12.0, Common 0.10.0): the Horizon look Harry picked on 6 Nov 2026, for the shell's own pages and every app that takes the kit from `KIT_URL`. Corners are square; buttons are keys; fields, checks, toggles and selects are wells with one focus ring; dialogs, notices, toasts, menus and tips are solid sheets; lists, tables, data marks and states sit in wells or on the horizon; and the needs-you colour and the typefaces are tokens. New parts for apps: combo box, multi select, number field, slider, time field and date range, search field, side sheet, info pop, snippet, folds, edit in place, first-run guide, files and media, people and activity, pickers and filter bar, run log, diff, inspector and the reading view. Nothing an app uses is removed. Aerie stays at 0.7, so the shell's band, Title and Orbit are unchanged.
