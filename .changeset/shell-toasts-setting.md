Settings has a new switch, Toasts for new notifications, which is off by default. When it's on, each new notification is also shown once as an Aerie toast at the bottom of the screen, with Open. When it's off, the shell behaves as it does today: it only pulses. Identity keeps the choice as the `toasts` preference (identity contract 1.13.0).
