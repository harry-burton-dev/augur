Folio makes a reader copy of every version: a new rendition worker (Python, with Docling) turns PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown and plain text into Markdown, with its pictures, a thumbnail of a PDF's first page, and the text for search. Versions say how their reader copy is coming along, the document dialog shows it, and a failed one can be asked for again. The service serves the reader copy, its pictures, the thumbnail and the text.
