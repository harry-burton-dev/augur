The standard unit intent (SDK 1.8): `UnitRef` and `isUnitRef`, `UNIT_OPEN` and `openUnit`, and `UNIT_LEVELS`. A unit is one place in a floor's library at any level (collection, subcollection, series, subseries or file). Artifacts handles `unit.open`, opening the library, the folder or the file.
