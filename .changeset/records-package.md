A new package, @augur/records: the rules every app's records share, for services and frontends alike. Levels a tree of records is built from, lifecycles (content and containers), read/edit/manage reaching down a tree, tags, relationships to records in any app, and publishing up the floors through a lifecycle filter with one owner. docs/app-patterns.md is the guide.
