DEFAULT_APPS names the apps a deployment installs from the app feed by default, once each, their asks agreed by the deployment; Admin marks them and refuses to remove one while it's named. A release on a contract this AUGUR no longer serves is refused in the spec's words, naming the app, its version and the contracts it needs. The mirror script copies contract 3 releases' part images.
