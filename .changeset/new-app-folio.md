Folio, the document libraries app, replaces the Library stub. Each floor keeps its own libraries, with folders up to six deep: anyone on the floor can see them and make folders, and the floor's leads make, rename and archive libraries. Folio has its own layout below the shell's top bar: a side rail with the floor's libraries and the open library's folders, and a list of what's inside.
