Artifacts moves what was published the old way onto publishing up the floors, by itself: a floor above gets the document published up showing Issued, anyone else it went to keeps it as a share, and the old publication ends on its record. Publishing one version to an audience is now only for a series' issues; any other document is published up the floors, and the reader no longer offers the old Publish for it.
