`ui-reader` shows Markdown as a page of text in the kit's type: every heading and top-level block gets a stable anchor, raw HTML is escaped, pictures load only through `imageSource`, and tables scroll in their own region. It has text sizes and a narrow or wide measure, reports its outline (`ui-outline`) and where the reader has got to (`ui-position`), and `goTo` takes you to an anchor. `ui-contents` lists an outline as links and marks the current one.
