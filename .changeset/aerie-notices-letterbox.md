Common gets `Letterbox`, the 2.39:1 frame for urgent things (PDR-009), with its ratio in the new `letterbox-ratio` token. Dialogs, lists and tips now sit above the shell's band through the layer tokens. Aerie gets its first parts: Toasts (they go by themselves after the new `motion-toast` token), AppUnavailable for an app that can't show yet, and Connection for offline, reconnecting and an ended session. The shell loads Aerie's `kit.css`.
