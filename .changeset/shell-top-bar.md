The shell's top bar is chrome, never a surface. On every screen it rests on a fade of the new `scrim` token over the water or the app, and Title's letterbox is a fade too, so the water shows through. Top left shows only the floor you're on, its icon and name, with no breadcrumb path. While an app is open, its name sits under the look-up handle (`area` adds the part of it you're in), and the handle takes the floor's colour. **Back** (`backTo`, `ui-back`) replaces All apps (`ui-home`): it goes up one level, the same as Esc, worked out from where you are rather than per app. The app's top edge fades in under the band, and the tab's title names the page, app and floor.
