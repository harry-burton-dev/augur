What building the app template on the app contract found: `appServer` checks the caller the gateway signs for the app's client, `app-<id>`; `startAppService` leaves registering to the app runner on contract 3; `appDatabaseFromEnv` reads the database a part's Score file asks for; `augur-app describe` and `publish` write release v2 from the Score files for an app on contract 3 (`augur-app release describe|publish` spells it out); the catalogue entry of a contract 3 app has its route, contract and intents.
