An app's pane keeps an inset in every layout: the shell draws it with Aerie's PaneLayout (kit 0.5.16), so the page heading and the app's screen share one inset from the pane's edges, sit clear of the rail, the About panel and the stage's fade, and the screen's parts are spaced with the kit's section gap. Compact density now comes from the kit's tokens rather than the shell's own override.
