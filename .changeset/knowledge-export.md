The knowledge graph exports a floor: the beliefs held in sight from it as Turtle or TriG, with PROV-O provenance back to the observations and sources they rest on (GET /floors/{floor}/export). A belief's evidence now lists the observations behind it that you can see (`evidence.seen`). With this every read draft 4 of the spec asks for is served, and the 24 competency questions pass over HTTP through the generated client.
