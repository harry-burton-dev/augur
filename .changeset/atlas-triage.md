Data Atlas Triage: proposals from a source (mentions and observations) are filed in the floor's Triage with suggested matches, and its leads decide what each mention names (one already known, a new one, or nothing) and whether each observation is accepted, kept as seen or dropped. Data Atlas handles `entity.propose`, asking what kind of thing the marked words name.
