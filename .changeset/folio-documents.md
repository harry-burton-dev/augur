Folio takes documents: drop PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown or text files (up to 200 MB) into a library or folder, and each is kept exactly as uploaded, as numbered versions with their size and SHA-256, in Folio's own bucket. A document opens to its versions, each one downloadable; whoever added it, or a lead, adds the next version, retitles or moves it, or deletes it. Uploads, versions, deletions and every download of an original go in the platform's audit log, and a download waits while the log can't be reached.
