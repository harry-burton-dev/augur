Activity can be filtered by target, person and kind (sign-ins or changes), downloaded as CSV or JSON lines (admins, at most 366 days at a time), keeps entries for the organisation's retention period (365 days by default, pruned nightly), and identity can remove a deleted person's personal values from it. Service kit adds `PERSONAL_DETAIL_KEYS` and `REMOVED`.
