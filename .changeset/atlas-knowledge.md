Data Atlas knows things about entities, fenced by floor: observations (what someone saw or read, with its source and grade, never changed), facts a floor's leads accept from them on two clocks (true from and until, and when recorded), links between entities, disputes, and raising an observation to a floor above. The entity page shows what's known, "true on" and "as recorded by" any day, and the subgraph now walks links.
