Artifacts' screens take on levels, lifecycles and publishing up: the places inside a library show as cards named by their level, each library or folder has its details and lifecycle, a version moves on from the reader, anything can be published up the floors (with the floors it can't go to saying why), and what was published up to a floor shows under From below. `GET /placements` now says where the caller could publish a record next.
