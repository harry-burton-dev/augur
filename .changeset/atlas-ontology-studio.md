Data Atlas ontology studio: an Ontology page everyone can read (kinds, details, kinds of link, packs and every version) where admins adopt terms from packs, decide the words sources asked for, and add or rename their own. A profile of the Common Core Ontologies comes with it; an org loads its own pack (the full CCO, MIP MIM) as OWL in Turtle. The ontology exports as OWL.
