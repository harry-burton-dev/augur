Series and level names are in Artifacts' new layout (PER-243). A series opens on its own page, with its cadence, who its issues go to, the next issue it expects, a place to drop that issue, and its issues listed. You can follow it there. Its leads can change it, or remove it while it has no issues. A collection's ring has **New series**. The front page's Late and Soon cards and a series tile now open the series in place, without going to the classic layout. **Level names** opens from the shelf's ring in a dialog, where the org's leads rename a level or give it its own name back.
