Kit 0.6.2: the new floor picker. A square box per floor on a rail, with a hard line where you are. It opens only after the pointer rests on it for a moment, drawing the wait as a line up its edge; tabbing in opens it at once. Pointing at a floor where something waits shows what it is, and that floor's box pulses (not under reduced motion).
