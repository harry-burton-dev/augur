Account is a dialog over the shell's menu, on kit 0.5.13. Its sections are tabs down the side, with a new Overview first (who you are, and an account check with Something's wrong? to secure the account). Each section's parts sit side by side while they fit and wait behind a switch when they don't, and long lists page, so nothing in it scrolls at 1280×720, 1024×768 or 800×600. It opens only from the menu, or on coming back from one of Keycloak's screens; an address alone no longer opens it.
