The knowledge graph takes seven more actions: `publish` (your own source, to a shared floor you are on, derived and not yet held), `dispute` and `settle`, `retract` (it falls from every floor the belief reached), `raise` (the floor above sees the belief; nothing is copied), `end` (a belief stopped being so, kept as history) and `forget` (your own floor only; the words are cleared and ledgered). Settle, retract, raise, end and forget are never granted to apps. Contract 1.8.0.
