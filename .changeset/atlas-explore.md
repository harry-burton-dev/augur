Data Atlas explorer: walk the graph around an entity a ring at a time, drawn with Sigma.js and laid out in a worker, with folds that open, hidden counts that say why, kinds, links, "True on" and "Hide disputed", and the same graph as a list. The subgraph keeps the strongest links first, always keeps its seeds, counts what lies past the last ring by relation and direction, says how many open disputes each link has, and says what each hidden entity is near.
