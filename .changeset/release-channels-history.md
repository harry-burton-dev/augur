Admin → Updates shows the history of every upgrade (who asked, from and to, how it ended, how long it took and the backup it took) and **What's new since** your version: every change between it and the newest release, once each, breaking ones first. Admin → Apps shows the same for each installed app: what's new in the waiting version and its history of installs. Releases carry each changed part's changelog (`changelogs` in the AUGUR release file) for this. Registry contract 1.24.0.
