Title shows what an app covers, not how to get around in it. An app may name up to six `areas` in its manifest (SDK 1.5), and each menu group its `area`. Title lists an app's areas, each opening its Orbit at the area's first group; an app with one area or none is a choice by itself, captioned with its description, so nothing is offered twice. Orbit keeps an area's groups together and names the area before the group ("Organisation / People"), and the area sits under the app's name below the handle. A group with one page is that page. Nothing repeats the label or icon of what it sits under: the registry warns when a manifest does (`menuWarnings`, also printed by `pnpm check:manifest`) and still accepts it. Admin has two areas, Organisation and System, with new kit icons `list`, `structure`, `server` and `gauge`. `ui-centre-menu` takes a category with no items as a choice by itself, and a category `section` that Orbit names before it.
