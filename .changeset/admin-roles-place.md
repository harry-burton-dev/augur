Admin has a Roles place: the roles made in AUGUR for the org and each department, each on a page of its own. An admin makes a role, gives it to people (until a day if they like) or to everyone in a department, says what it gives on a department or the org, and takes any of it back with a reason. The permissions service's role answer now says what a role can give on a floor and on the org.
