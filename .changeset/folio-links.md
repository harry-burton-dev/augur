Artifacts links records: a library, folder or document can relate to, supersede or respond to another, be about a thing in Data Atlas, or serve an intent in Mandate (`@augur/records`' relations). Links read both ways, a linked record you can't open stays listed and says so, and the reader and a library's or folder's details have a Links section to add, open and remove them.
