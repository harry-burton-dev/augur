Folio shares libraries and documents. A library lets in everyone who can stand on its floor, from wherever they are, and whoever manages it can let in a person, a team, a team and the teams below it, or the whole org, to read, edit or manage it, or just one document of it. Folio asks identity who each person stands for (the new `people:read` scope) and writes down each document's readers, so every list keeps to what the person may see. The Share dialog finds people and teams by name, and what's shared from other floors is under "Shared with you". Every change to who can see something is on the record.
