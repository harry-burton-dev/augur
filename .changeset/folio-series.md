Series: a named run of documents in a library on a cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly or now and then), with the tags and audience each issue starts with. The next issue is added from one file, numbered, named for its period and published; people follow a series to be told of each issue, its owner is reminded when one is late, and the reader moves between issues.
