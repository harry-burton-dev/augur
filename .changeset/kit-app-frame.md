App kit, part 2: the frame. New `ui-app-frame` (the slate ground, a rail, the main column and an aside), `ui-page-head` (the trail of what a page is inside, its title, one line of summary, and two actions with the rest in ⋯) and `ui-section` (a labelled part with its count, one action and a why, folding after five). New token `measure-rail` (240px). Type tokens are now emitted in rem, so a person's browser text size scales them. Artifacts, Mandate, Data Atlas and Auspex use the frame and page head instead of their own grids, heads and trails.
