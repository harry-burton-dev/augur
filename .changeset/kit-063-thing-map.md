Kit 0.6.3 (app-kit 0.23.0): apps can draw the map of what is around a thing, `quill-thing-map`. The thing sits in the middle with what it touches round it, grouped, each line read from its side; past twelve it says how many more. A thing's page already offers it ("See how it connects") when the app listens. Data Atlas is the first to use it.
