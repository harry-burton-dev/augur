App kit, part 4: references. New `ui-ref` (a thing from any app as a chip: its kind's icon, its name, a floor dot when it's kept on another floor; it opens where you are, and can be removed) and `ui-refs` (a row of them, counting the ones you can't open with where they're kept and who to ask). Mandate's bound entities and context documents, and Data Atlas's links in the explorer and Triage's outcome, use them instead of underlined text buttons.
