The knowledge graph takes `merge`, `split` and `retire` on a floor settler's word, and `undo` for your own retain, hold or keep: undoing retain retracts what it kept, undoing hold withdraws the belief from every floor it reached and lets what it replaced stand again (or takes back only the evidence it added), and undoing keep flags the observation again. An action's record says whether it was undone. Migration 007; contract 1.9.0.
