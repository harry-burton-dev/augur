AUGUR is an app platform built to be deployed many times. A shell in the browser loads separately built apps, and behind one gateway sit AUGUR's services: identity, the app registry, events, storage, models and the updater.

This section will hold an overview, the concepts you need (floors, the shell, the gateway, platform and pillar apps, the app contract, events), two quickstarts and a glossary. Until those pages are written, start from the guides in the repository:

- [Running AUGUR on your own server](https://github.com/harry-burton-dev/augur/blob/main/docs/deploy.md)
- [Build an app](https://github.com/harry-burton-dev/augur/blob/main/docs/build-an-app.md)
