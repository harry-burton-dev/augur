A design lab run from 3 November 2026, the first steered prompt of batch 4, “the services underneath the screens”: how AUGUR should decide **who is told, how and when**. It covers who gets told, on which channel and in what order, what happens when nobody answers, and how a person shapes it. Sources: telephone switchboards and exchange operators, the postal service (classes, recorded delivery, redirection, “while you were out” cards), hospital on-call, bleeps and SBAR handover, PagerDuty-style escalation policies, air traffic sector handoff and flight progress strips, and newspaper editions.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (routing sheet, why was I told, my switchboard, escalation, the app owner’s view, digests) and four combined screens. All of them run on **one routing model** (`_src/lib.js`) over **one week of one team’s notices** (`_src/data.js`): Westbrook District Council’s Estates repairs team, 41 notices from five apps, with each person’s hours, away and cover, mutes, the on-call rota and the team’s escalation policy. Every route, hold, receipt and escalation step on every page is worked out by the rules, never typed. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows the technical words, and each page lists its own keys.

Start with [The week replayed](combined/v1-the-week-replayed.html) (the required replay of a week through the rules) and [Why, and change it here](combined/v2-why-and-change-it.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`. Sources for every page are in `_src/` (`python3 _src/build.py` rebuilds them).
