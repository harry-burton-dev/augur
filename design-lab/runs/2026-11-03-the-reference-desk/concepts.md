# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Pencil and ink (`pencil-and-ink`)
Anything a model proposes is pencil; facts and counts are always ink. A guess never enters a count, becomes fact only through Triage and keeps who proposed it; answer sentences come from templates, not the model.

Evidence: ask V5, example V2 and V6, workings V6, cant V5, grammar V3 and V6, combined V2. Could steer: the apprentice (Q), the briefing (M), Mandate drafting.

## Ask only what changes (`ask-only-what-changes`)
A clarifying question appears only when its readings give different rows, biggest split first; a loose word may stay loose and is saved with the question.

Evidence: ask V5, V1, V4, combined V1. Could steer: the index (P), Mandate asks, the form (X).

## The answer has a base (`the-answer-has-a-base`)
Every answer says what share of what it covers, counts every record on exactly one line, and shows the sum.

Evidence: cant V1, workings V2, grammar V4, combined V3. Could steer: the briefing (M), Explorer, the meter (W4).

## The question is the record (`the-question-is-the-record`)
A saved question keeps words, choices, steps, time and access, never rows; share the question, not the answer; reruns never call the model.

Evidence: saved V2, V5, V6, example V6, combined V1. Could steer: the edition (S), the minute (T), the case file (U).

## Five reasons an answer moves (`five-reasons-an-answer-moves`)
Every change to a saved answer names one of five reasons: something happened, we learned about the past, different access, a guess was settled, a word changed meaning.

Evidence: saved V6, combined V1. Could steer: the switchboard (O), watch for change (M).

## The empty knows its errand (`the-empty-knows-its-errand`)
Each way to be empty sends its own errand (Triage job, share request, Mandate ask), previewed before sending; no record says nobody can close it.

Evidence: cant V2 and V4, combined V3. Could steer: the groundskeeper (K), the commission (V).

Also advanced: the workings show (now explored), said in a sentence (ask by example) and six ways to be empty (errands).
