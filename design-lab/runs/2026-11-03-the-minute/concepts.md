# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Status beside the words (`status-beside-the-words`)
A record's words are agreed once and never edited. Its status (in force, worth revisiting, amended, reversed, complete, lapsed) is computed from dates, watches and later records, and every place that cites it reads that status live, at word size. A correction is a seam; a change of mind is a new record that points back. Evidence: grammar V1 and V4, record V1 and V5, combined V2. Could steer: the edition (S), the case file (U), the form (X), any record other apps cite.

## Agreed in the room (`agreed-in-the-room`)
Minutes are agreed as they are made. The line is read back, and each person present answers "that is what we decided". That assent to the record is kept apart from agreement with the decision (the vote), so someone who abstained still confirms the minute is right. The model may hear and draft in pencil; only the room inks. Evidence: live V4 and V2, combined V3. Could steer: approvals, the commission (V) sign-off, any record made by several people at once.

## Decided versus said (`decided-versus-said`)
As in a court judgment (ratio and obiter), only what was decided binds, carries a status and is found as a decision. What was said along the way is kept as context, and what was **assumed** gets its own lane and is watched, because that is what decisions usually fail on. Evidence: grammar V3 and V1, record V3 and V4. Could steer: Mandate discussion threads, meeting notes, the reference desk's answers.

## Revisit lines (`revisit-lines`)
Every decision states at the table what would reopen it: a line on a record it rests on, a date, an assumption. AUGUR watches them. Crossing one marks the decision "worth revisiting" and asks the person who chairs once; a watch never reverses or suspends anything. A decision with no line stays in force until someone remembers it. Evidence: revisit V1, record V2, grammar V6, combined V1. Could steer: Mandate intents, Upgrade Centre policies, care is scheduled, the groundskeeper (K).

## The wake (`the-wake`)
A decision has a wake: the actions it set, the documents that cite it, the forecasts that count it, the later decisions that lean on it. AUGUR can know the wake because each holds a reference to the decision. Revisiting shows the wake before the vote. Reversing turns it into seams and named jobs, and routes the news to exactly the owners it touches, with nobody else told. Evidence: revisit V2, V3 and V6, record V3, combined V1 and V2. Could steer: the switchboard (O), the edition (S), Upgrade Centre (what rests on a setting), Data Atlas (what rests on a fact).

Also advanced: question and answer (actions as asked, accepted, answered, closed; done is not closed), one now (one rail for a decision's life and the floor's actions), time as an address (in force on a date, `@date`), the workings show (the log's answers built from records), the decision sets the bar (a standard of proof per kind, with rules per kind for revisiting), visible repair (moved dates, minute corrections and reversal seams), pencil and ink (heard in pencil, agreed in ink), consequence preview (the wake before the vote), watches say what they can't see (the revisit watch's list).

Related concepts from parallel runs: status beside the words sits next to *status is a mark* (paper and the desk); agreed in the room next to *the readback moves it*; the wake next to *copies know their source* (the label). Record V4 advances *the warrant shows*.
