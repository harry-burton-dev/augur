A design lab run of 3 November 2026, prompt T from batch 5, "work outputs": how a **decision** and the **actions** that follow it become first-class outputs in AUGUR. Each is recorded once, with who decided, why, what it rests on, who does what by when, and what later overturned it. Sources: board minutes and resolutions (company secretary practice), Hansard and the agreed record, court judgments (ratio and obiter, headnotes, citators), decision records (ADRs, AUGUR's own), action and RAID logs, plus Robert's Rules, Toulmin's model of argument, back-of-book indexing and point-in-time legislation.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (the minute sheet, minuting live, a decision record, actions out of decisions, revisiting, the decision log) and four combined screens. All sample data is one organisation's Fleet and Facilities leadership meeting over a year. The story every page shares is **D-118**: on 12 May 2026 the meeting moved North depot van inspections in-house as a six-month trial and set three actions. On 3 November it was reversed by D-131. Every status and state is computed from the records' dates, so any page can be read "as at" another date.

Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows the record fields, and **G** turns the model gateway off where the model has a part (nothing in the record changes). Start with [From the table to the reversal](combined/v1-table-to-reversal.html) (← → step through six months) and [The decision record](combined/v2-the-record.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`. Sources for every page are in `_src/` (`python3 _src/build.py` rebuilds them).
