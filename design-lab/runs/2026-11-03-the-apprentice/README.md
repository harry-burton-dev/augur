A design lab run of 3 November 2026, from batch 4, "the services underneath": how an **agent should behave as a colleague** in AUGUR. What it may do on its own, what it must ask about, how its work is checked, how trust in it grows or shrinks, and how a person sees what it did while they were away. It builds on the model gateway (every call audited first, a daily limit per app, off until an admin turns it on) and the agent runtime that ADR 0014 still has as Proposed. Sources: apprenticeship and the guild ladder, clinical delegation (the five rights), Sheridan and Verplank's levels of automation and SAE J3016, the clerk of works' site diary and the master's night order book, aviation's flight mode annunciator and the research on automation surprises, stage management's standby and go, acceptance sampling, permits to work, the surgical time out, product recalls by lot, just culture and Toyota's andon cord.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (the apprentice sheet, the site diary, a proposal waiting, scope and level, standby and go, a mistake) and four combined screens. All sample data is one port authority: Survey reader reads vessel survey reports and proposes details to Data Atlas under Ana Ferreira; Filing clerk files certificates under Tom Okoye; Notice drafter drafts Notices to Mariners under Rhiannon Price, the harbourmaster. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows the ADR 0014 words under the plain ones, and **G** turns the model gateway off where the model has a part. Start with [A week in Triage](combined/v1-a-week-in-triage.html) and [From standby to go](combined/v3-standby-to-go.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`.

Every page was checked by screenshot at 1600×900 and 1280×800 with a script that also reports console errors and sideways scroll. Pages are built by `_src/build.py` from the shared `_src/common.css`, `_src/data.js` and `_src/lib.js` and each surface's `_src/v*.src.html`; the built pages link the kit as the brief says.
