# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Works under someone (`works-under-someone`)
Every agent has a named supervisor and deputy, an errand in a sentence, a scope and limits, shown wherever its work appears and readable by anyone it affects. The supervisor answers for its work; no supervisor, no run. Evidence: grammar V1 and V5, scope V6, combined V4. Could steer: keys and doors (N), the switchboard (O), any service acting for a person.

## The maker's mark (`makers-mark`)
Work made by an agent carries its monogram: dotted while it is pencil, solid once a person accepts it, and kept for good with "accepted by …". It tells agent work from people's at text size, in any app, without a robot icon. Evidence: grammar V4, proposal V6 and V1, combined V1. Could steer: the hallmark (W5), the label (L), Artifacts provenance.

## Now and next (`now-and-next`)
Wherever automation works, one line says what it is doing now, what it will do next, what will stop it, and why it last changed; a changed cell is boxed once, then the line is still. "Holding", "Stopped" and "Paused" are modes in words, never blanks. Evidence: grammar V6, diary V4, combined V1 and V2. Could steer: the Upgrade Centre (rollouts), the switchboard (O), jobs in Admin.

## Silence is not a go (`silence-is-not-a-go`)
An agent does every reversible part of a job itself and holds at the first step it can't take back. A standby has five states (standby, standing by, go, hold, lapsed), a go-by and a deputy asked by rule; acknowledging is not a yes, and a standby nobody answers lapses, not done. Evidence: standby V5, V1 and V6, combined V3. Could steer: approvals, the Letterbox, Upgrade Centre windows, Mandate deadlines.

## Trust changes the sample, not the signature (`trust-changes-the-sample`)
A person signs every fact an agent proposes; the rung only decides how much of the work they look at first. Batches are signed by a sample drawn by rule across sources and their layouts, read-once details are always sampled, and the sample is recorded on every detail it let through. Evidence: diary V5, proposal V4, combined V1, mistake V3. Could steer: Triage at volume, imports, the meter (W4).

## Trust by the kind (`trust-by-the-kind`)
Autonomy is set per kind of work, not per agent: four rungs with no rung above 3, a cap per kind set by AUGUR (facts for Data Atlas cap at 2), and the supervisor's rung within it. A widening needs evidence from work people already checked (a replay), and a mistake narrows only the kind that failed. Evidence: grammar V2, scope V1 and V5, mistake V5, combined V1 and V4. Could steer: keys and doors (N), the constitution (R), app scopes in Admin.

## Trust lapses (`trust-lapses`)
Every widening is a permit with an end date. A person renews it with the record in front of them, or the kind of work drops back to asking, quietly and safely. An agent nobody watches drifts back to asking, never forward to acting. Evidence: scope V3, combined V4. Could steer: keys and doors (N, access recertification), feature flags, app grants.

## Mistakes have siblings (`mistakes-have-siblings`)
An agent's errors are systematic, so one found means more made the same way. A flag recalls the lot (same agent, kind, source, layout, runs), each repair leaves a seam, and the review asks why the checks missed it before what to change. Evidence: mistake V2, V1 and V3, combined V1. Could steer: Data Atlas imports, the case file (U), the edition (S, errata).

## Stop the line (`stop-the-line`)
Anyone who can see an agent's work can pause one kind of it at once, with their name and a reason; nothing is undone, and only the supervisor restarts it. The smallest safe action belongs to everyone affected, the decision to the person accountable. Evidence: mistake V6, grammar V5, combined V1. Could steer: approvals, rollouts in the Upgrade Centre, the switchboard (O).

Also advanced: pencil and ink (agent work is pencil until a person accepts it, and keeps its mark after), consequence preview (the rung preview, the undo's reach, the cost of a lapse), the workings show (the method card with the model's one step), visible repair (now explored: seams on repaired facts with the whole chain named), question and answer (now explored: standby, standing by, go), reach before act (corrections go where the wrong number was read), waiting is a place (held for you, one by one) and dark when normal ("nobody was called").
