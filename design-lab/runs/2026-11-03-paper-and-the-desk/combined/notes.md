# Round 2 · Combined screens

Four screens built from the round 1 winners. All share the run's one `DATA` object and are built from `../_src/combined/v*.src.html`. Each was checked at 1600×900 and 1280×800, with every hinted key pressed and with reduced motion. None has console errors or sideways scroll.

## C1: The handover, both sides (`v1-handover-both-sides.html`)
*The required screen.* One handover end to end, from both desks at once. Amara's desk is on the left, Tom's on the right, and the strip's token sits between them. A five-step track runs across the top: **card** 10:44, **offer** 10:44, **read back** 10:51, **answer** 10:55, **take** 10:58. N and B step, 1 to 5 jump, and R replays.
- **Amara's side:** her bay with M-207 selected, and the SBAR card under it. S and B say "from records"; A and R say "Amara's words". On offer, her strip turns dashed with AO → TR: still hers.
- **Tom's side:** his bay. M-207 arrives dashed as "offered to you by Amara Osei". Under it are her card's first line, then his readback in his own words, his one question ("Has Helen seen the 13 Nov date yet?"), her answer, and "It is mine from 10:58".
- **Between:** the token moves from AO to "AO + TR" in the zone, then to TR.
- **Foot:** both stamp books and the thing's own account each gain their line at the step that writes it.

*From.* Handover V1 (SBAR card, readback), V2 (resting on the line), V3 (the zone), V4 (one token); ledger V3 (given and taken written twice); approvals V5 (stamp book).

*What works.* Seen from both sides, "offered is not moved" needs no explanation: during steps 2 to 4 both desks show the strip and both say whose it is. The question and answer happen on the card, so they stay with the thing for its next holder. Nothing glows or blurs. The only motion is the token moving once per step.

*What fails or risks.* Showing both desks is a teaching view; in the product each person sees their own side. At 1280 the B box and the fourth strip in each bay are dropped to fit. The SBAR's S and B text is a template filled from records. If the model gateway drafts them they must be pencil until Amara accepts (*pencil and ink*).

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 5

## C2: The bay and the stamp (`v2-the-bay-and-the-stamp.html`)
Home as the strip bay (home V1). Selecting the approval opens the back of the strip as the place to act:
- the stamp row in order (approvals V1);
- **what you sign for**, with ticks and initials per section and "nobody" against section 6 (approvals V2);
- three stamps: initial, initial and note, return (approvals V6).

The moment picks "Initialled, noting", the note naming section 6 and Joe Park. Amara's stamp lands, and the strip leaves her bay for Helen's ("A-118 passed to Helen · stub 42").

*What works.* An approval stops being a separate inbox. It is a strip like everything else, and the stamp is something you do on its back. The cocked M-207 stays put while the approval leaves, so the bay never reorders around you.

*What fails or risks.* The back pane is dense at 1280. The scope ticks should default to what you were asked, so the common case is one press.

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 5

## C3: The team wall (`v3-the-team-wall.html`)
The Team floor's board:
- a row per person with where they are (board V5);
- a slot per job up to the team's limit (board V1);
- the round's mark on every card, with initials (board V6);
- away hatching, and nobody's job waiting in the side column.

The moment: Nkechi offers Grace's leak trace, which is due today, to Kai, who has room. It shows dashed in both rows, "offered to KW by NB" and "offered from GI · take it to hold it". A dashed note warns about Tom's two jobs due while he is away.

*What works.* The tally (13 free slots · 3 need help · 1 held by nobody) is the whole board in one line. The side column is the only thing that asks for attention, and it asks in words. Cover goes through the same offer and readback as any handover.

*What fails or risks.* At 1280 the mark words truncate. Presence and per-person load on one wall need Harry's decision on what a team sees about each other.

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 4

## C4: Nothing erased (`v4-nothing-erased.html`)
A correction followed to everything that relied on the old value. A line draws through 2014, and 2017 is written beside it with Amara's initials, the time and Lena's countersign (ledger V2). The reason sits under it. The account gains entry 6 (ledger V1). On the right, both citing documents get a line ("This document quoted 2014"). The one with a sign-off, Joe Park's approval of the gas safety plan, shows its stamp, what it was **signed for** ("the boiler ages, using F-3301 (2014)"), a dashed "covered a value since corrected" mark, and two ways to answer: re-initial for 2017, or a note saying no change is needed.

*What works.* This is where "say what a mark covers" pays off. Because Joe's stamp said what it covered, AUGUR can tell him exactly which part moved, without voiding his approval or changing his document. "He decides; his answer is a new mark beside the old one" is additive marks all the way through.

*What fails or risks.* It needs Artifacts to know which facts a document quotes (the Artifacts guide has "about" links to Data Atlas, not per-value quotes). A correction to a much-cited fact would raise many marks, so the owner's view needs grouping.

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 5 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 4
