# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Someone holds it (`someone-holds-it`)
Every thing that needs someone has exactly one named holder at a time, shown on it, and is never held by nobody. A thing nobody holds waits in plain sight with how long it has waited. One holder is also a server rule (one token, moved in one step), not just a label. A holder who is away stays the holder, and that is shown, until the work is handed on.

Evidence: grammar V1 and V6, handover V4, board V1 and V5, ledger V3, combined V1 and V3. Sources: flight progress strips, railway token working, T-card boards. Could steer: keys and doors (N), the switchboard (O), Mandate ownership, Admin.

## The readback moves it (`the-readback-moves-it`)
A handover is an offer, not a move. It carries a fixed card (SBAR: situation and background from records, assessment and recommendation in the giver's words) and moves only when the taker reads it back in their own words. In the changeover zone both names show, both can read and ask, and the giver still answers. Nothing is reassigned by a timer. Pointing out (they see it, you keep it) is a different verb from handing over.

Evidence: handover V1, V3, V5 and V6, home V2, combined V1. Sources: SBAR, I-PASS (Starmer 2014), the radar handoff and point-out, relay changeover zones. Could steer: the apprentice (Q, handing work to a model), the kitchen brigade (J), Mandate delegation.

## Say what you sign for (`say-what-you-sign-for`)
Every initial and stamp carries its scope (which sections, which claim), built from ticks, never typed. The row always names what nobody has signed for. Because marks say what they covered, a later correction can tell exactly the stamps it touches, without voiding them.

Evidence: approvals V1, V2 and V4, combined V2 and V4. Sources: checked-by and approved-by blocks, good documentation practice, contract page initials. Could steer: the hallmark (W5), the commission (V), the edition (S).

## Shared work stays sharp (`shared-work-stays-sharp`)
Ink (initials, outline, position) carries who holds a thing and its state. Light carries urgency only. Depth may lift one person's own focus, but never blurs work another person holds, because peripheral sight of a neighbour's work is how a handover is seen coming. This argues against using focal planes on shared views.

Evidence: grammar V5, home V2, board V1, combined V1. Sources: MacKay 1999, Wears and Perry 2007. Could steer: control room (E), game interfaces (G), any shared view.

## Set aside, not flagged (`set-aside-not-flagged`)
A person can cock a thing: step it out of line by one fixed distance with a private note, as a controller cocks a strip. It is a reminder by position, with no priority, due date or signal to others. It is private, it leaves no mark in the shared record, and nothing moves at rest.

Evidence: home V1, V3 and V6, grammar V1 and V3, combined V2. Sources: cocked flight strips (MacKay 1999). Could steer: Home, the field notebook (W3), attention as wear.

## Room you can see (`room-you-can-see`)
Capacity is drawn as empty slots by the team's own rule (who set it, when), and overload as work sticking out below the rim, never squeezed in or hidden. The limit is a team setting, never a personal target, and a full slot informs the giver rather than refusing the work.

Evidence: board V1 and V4, home V4, combined V3. Sources: T-card boards, kanban, the heijunka box. Could steer: the meter (W4), mise en place (J), Upgrade Centre windows.

## Status is a mark (`status-is-a-mark`)
"On track" or "needs help" is a person's dated, initialled mark from a round, not a colour that changes by itself. A card the round didn't reach says "not checked", never looks fine.

Evidence: board V6, combined V3. Sources: kamishibai boards, the ward round. Could steer: the groundskeeper (K), the minute (T), Mandate status.

Also advanced:
- *visible repair* (strike in place, the four-part mark, the correction slip);
- *history as a material* (an account with "value after");
- *mine versus ours* (private cocks; a stamp book that is private as a whole while each mark stays public);
- *waiting is a place* (offered work rests on the line);
- *the honest blank* ("not checked this round").

It argued against *focal planes* for shared work.
