# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Told, not asked (`told-not-asked`)
A change inside an intent's freedom, by someone who has said that intent back, needs nobody's yes. It becomes a notice to the floor above that says how it still serves the intent (built from what was checked, never typed), with "Seen" and "Ask a question"; a question undoes nothing. Anything outside freedom still asks the named holder of that choice, and anything across a limit asks the limit's owner. Approvals shrink to the decisions that really belong above. Evidence: within V6 and V2, combined V1 and V4; mission command's disciplined initiative ("act, then report"), schemes of delegation. Could steer: approvals, keys and doors (N), the Inbox (ADR 0026), Home.

## Said back is the key (`said-back-is-the-key`)
An intent passed down is an open question to each holder until they say it back in their own words and its owner confirms or corrects it. Freedom within the intent unlocks only for confirmed holders: a new arrival on rotation, or a team that hasn't said it back, still asks. The words show what was understood, and what wasn't said is shown to the owner before confirming. Evidence: backbrief V1 and V3, grammar V4, within V6 (Rita's request still asks), combined V1; Bungay's backbrief, ADP 6-0's confirmation brief. Could steer: keys and doors (N), onboarding and rotation, handover.

## Four verdicts (`four-verdicts`)
Every option set against an intent gets one of four verdicts, by rule from the record: within; within but against a key task (allowed, with the cost said in the intent's own units); ask a named person (outside freedom); across a limit (only its owner can lift it). Nothing is filtered out of sight: the limit bin is shown with its owner. "Within, against a task" is the honest middle that approval schemes lack. Evidence: grammar V6, within V1 and V5, combined V1 and V2; Bratman's intention as a filter on options. Could steer: approvals, the Upgrade Centre (rollout choices), agents' proposals.

## Correct the binding, not the order (`correct-the-binding`)
What reaches a person is ordered by a fixed rule from the intent parts each item is bound to (a limit and your own part first; untouched folded, never hidden), and every item can show the trace: tag, record, part, rule. If the order is wrong, a binding is wrong; you correct the binding (for everyone it reaches), never drag an item up or down. A model may only suggest a binding for an untagged item, in pencil. Evidence: situation V1, V6 and V4, combined V2; the workings show, pencil and ink. Could steer: Home "what needs you", notifications, search ranking, the switchboard (O).

## Branches agreed early (`branches-agreed-early`)
The cheapest change of plan is one someone already agreed: a branch written into the intent in catchball ("if Ashby is short, Ryecroft first, then Thornley for up to 72 hours"), with a cap that is a signal ahead, not a timer. Drafts are rehearsed on last year's real surprises before they are passed down, so gaps are found while they cost a line, not a 03:00 phone call. Evidence: within V4, writing V3, V4 and V6, combined V3; ADP 5-0 branches and sequels, rehearsal of concept, Klein's premortem. Could steer: the Upgrade Centre (rollback plans), Mandate, runbooks in Artifacts.

## Two drifts (`two-drifts`)
Work drifts from an intent (worked out from records: the share of hours on a key task, a rate against the line) and an intent drifts from the world (an assumption it rests on stops holding). The first moves the needle, the second moves the line; the first is a question about the work, the second about the intent. Each is asked once of its owner, at the crossing, in a muted warm tone, never in the Letterbox, and "amend the intent" is always one of the answers. Evidence: backbrief V4, V5 and V6, combined V4; cross-track error in navigation, assumption-based planning, the minute's revisit lines. Could steer: Data Atlas watches, the briefing's forecasts, Home.

Also advanced: **intent travels two levels** (now explored: the record, the nest rule "two up in full, further up folded", the rank rule, the backbrief and freedom; nest V1 and V6, combined V1), **consequence preview** (cost in the intent's units: 6 Ml is 0.07 points of the Org's end state; within V1 and V3), **question and answer** (passed down is a question, said back answers it; notices, asks and asked-once are three different kinds), **strictest wins** (limits only tighten going down the nest, each names who can lift it; nest V5, writing V4), **purpose is the key** (a derived grant follows the intent's freedom and the backbrief; within V6), **revisit lines** (assumptions on intents watched and asked once; grammar V4, backbrief V5), **pencil and ink** (a model sorts leftover sentences, drafts a missing freedom line, binds untagged items, matches backbrief phrases, all in pencil), **visible repair** (catchball changes kept as named seams), **focal planes** (the nest racks focus floor by floor), **silence is not a go** (a branch's cap is a signal, not a timer) and **context is a working set** (context made relative to a reader by their intents, with the why on every item).
