A design lab run of 3 November 2026, the last step of batch 6, "thinking made visible": how AUGUR could **apply intent to context**. Mandate holds intents; this run asks how an intent becomes useful when it meets a situation: what reaches a person ranked by the intents it touches, how their work nests under the intents two levels above, and what they may change without asking, with the reason recorded. Sources: mission command (Moltke's *Auftragstaktik*, US ADP 6-0 and ADP 5-0's branches and sequels), Stephen Bungay's *The Art of Action* (the backbrief and the three gaps), Gary Klein's intent statements and premortem, Hoshin Kanri's catchball and X-matrix, OKRs, Bratman's belief-desire-intention model, the five-paragraph order, schemes of delegation, and cross-track error from navigation.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (the intent sheet, writing an intent, the nest, intent meets the situation, acting within intent, the backbrief and drift) and four combined screens. All sample data is one regional water company after a dry summer: the Org intent is to get through the winter without household restrictions; Network Operations rests the Thornley reservoirs; North Zone feeds itself at night through the Kelda link; and Ellis Marsh, a network controller, keeps that feed full. At 06:10 a quality alert takes a borehole out and tonight's plan is 8 Ml/d short. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows the doctrine words under the plain ones, and **G** turns the model gateway off where a model has a part (it only ever writes in pencil). Start with [From the Org to Ellis, and back](combined/v1-org-to-ellis.html) and [Ellis's morning](combined/v2-ellis-morning.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`.

Every page was checked by screenshot at 1600×900 and 1280×800 with a script that also reports console errors, sideways scroll and clipped boxes. Pages are built by `_src/build.py` from the shared `_src/common.css`, `_src/data.js` and `_src/lib.js` and each surface's `_src/v*.src.html`; the built pages link the kit as the brief says.
