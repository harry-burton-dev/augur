The sixth design lab run (3 November 2026), the third of batch 3, "data for operators": how AUGUR **presents and abstracts** data so that non-technical people can use it, from a single fact to a briefing a manager reads in a minute, with the right detail for each reader. Sources: the Neuraths' Isotype and the transformer, BLUF and staff briefing practice, Rosling and Our World in Data, Bret Victor's ladder of abstraction, the IPCC's calibrated language and the UK probability yardstick, and Tufte's sparklines and small multiples.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (briefing sheet, the fact at three heights, the one-minute briefing, choosing the picture, confidence for everyone, from finding to action) and four combined screens. All sample data is a regional services organisation. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **G** turns the model gateway off (no number changes) and **T** shows the technical names. Start with [Three readers](combined/v1-three-readers.html) and [From question to briefing](combined/v2-from-question-to-briefing.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`.
