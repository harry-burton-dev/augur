# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Same digits at every height (`same-digits-at-every-height`)
Headline, picture and records come from one query; a headline whose heights disagree can't be published. Evidence: grammar V3, heights V1 and V3, combined V1. Could steer: the edition (S), Explorer.

## Confidence is worked out (`confidence-is-worked-out`)
Yardstick words come from grades and agreement, never typed; a person may only lower one; counts, disputes and gaps take no word; a summary is no surer than its weakest claim. Evidence: confidence V4 and V1, grammar V2, combined V4. Could steer: the label (L), the minute (T).

## Question, not chart (`question-not-chart`)
Operators pick a question, never a chart type; one picture and one sentence template per question; the never list ships with reasons. Evidence: picture V6 and V1, heights V6, combined V2. Could steer: Explorer, the meter (W4), Home.

## The reader's minute (`the-readers-minute`)
Each part has a reading-time budget; cuts drop into "if asked", never deleted. Evidence: minute V5 and V3, grammar V6, combined V2. Could steer: Home, the edition (S), the case file (U).

## Reach before act (`reach-before-act`)
Every publish, feed or watch first shows who reads it on each floor and what they can open, with each reader's own copy as the proof. Evidence: action V4 and V3, combined V3. Could steer: keys and doors (N), the switchboard (O).

## Watches say what they can't see (`watches-say-what-they-cant-see`)
A watch lists the events that would move it and marks those AUGUR can't notice yet. Evidence: action V5, combined V3. Could steer: the switchboard (O), the groundskeeper (K).

## The decision sets the bar (`the-decision-sets-the-bar`)
A decision carries a standard of proof, and claims sort into clear, below the bar, and needs someone first. Needs Harry's decision. Evidence: confidence V4, combined V4. Could steer: Mandate, the minute (T).

Also advanced: the overview rung (now explored), pencil and ink (one pencil slot), hear the reach (seen as a picture).
