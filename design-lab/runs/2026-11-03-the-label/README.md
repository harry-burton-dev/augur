A design lab run (3 November 2026), from batch 3: **governance you can see on the data**. Who may see, use and share a value, how sensitive it is, where it came from and how long it is kept travel with the value itself, the way food carries its allergens and a government paper carries its marking, so operators make the right call without reading the policy. Sources: UK Government Security Classifications (banner and portion marks), food labelling (allergen emphasis, front-of-pack), the Dataset Nutrition Label and Datasheets for Datasets, Creative Commons deeds, evidence chain of custody, Kantara consent receipts, plus GHS hazard labels, FOI redaction and archive closure periods.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (label sheet, the label on a record, not for you, sharing across floors, a dataset's own label, custody) and four combined screens. All sample data is one port authority: the Marlow Star grounding, the pilot's time on duty (OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE · PERSONAL), a briefing on fatigue and groundings, and the Pilotage safety records. Every marking word, use and rule comes from the deployment's policy object. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows the admin words, and **S** swaps in a second deployment's policy (INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, RESTRICTED) on every page, with no code change. Every marking opens its deed in a click. Start with [One fact, four places](combined/v1-one-fact-four-places.html), which follows one sensitive fact from a source document, through Data Atlas and an answer, into a shared briefing. The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`. The sources for every page are in `_src/` (one `data.js`, `common.css` and `lib.js` for the whole run; `python3 _src/build.py` builds the pages, `node _src/check.js OUTDIR files…` screenshots and checks them).
