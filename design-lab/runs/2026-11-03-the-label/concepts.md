# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Said another way (`said-another-way`)
A label rarely needs to deny a value. Its caveat says it another way for each reader (a name becomes "a pilot", a figure becomes part of a count, a value becomes a link that opens per reader), and the place it appears says which rule did it. Evidence: record V2 and V5, not for you V2, combined V1 and V2. Could steer: Explorer answers, Artifacts quoting, notifications.

## The strictest part wins, and says so (`strictest-wins-and-says-why`)
When values meet, the strictest marking wins, uses narrow and each part keeps its own keep-until, and the result always names the part that raised it and the way back down (remove it, say it as a count, quote it as a link). The one exception (a count over the minimum group drops PERSONAL) is a rule with an owner, shown as a change. Evidence: record V1, sharing V1 and V2, combined V1 and V3. Could steer: Artifacts publishing, the edition (S), the case file (U).

## Purpose is the key (`purpose-is-the-key`)
Nobody asks for "access". They ask to use something for a purpose, until a date, and the purpose is checked against the label as they choose it. The grant, the share and the consent all carry it, and each leaves a receipt both sides keep. Evidence: not for you V1, V4 and V6, sharing V2 and V5, custody V4, combined V2. Could steer: keys and doors (N), approvals, the switchboard (O).

## Four kinds of no (`four-kinds-of-no`)
"Not for you" is always one of four answers: you may; ask someone, by name; only the person it's about; no one can, with the clause and whoever owns the policy. Each has a different next step, and none of them is "access denied". Evidence: not for you V4 and V6, combined V2. Could steer: keys and doors (N), Admin, every locked slot in the kit.

## Enforce what you can see (`enforce-what-you-can-see`)
AUGUR enforces the parts of a label it can watch happen: who opens a value, where it is shared, whether it reaches the model gateway, whether it leaves the organisation, how long it is kept. For purposes it can't observe (appraisal, say), it asks, records the declared purpose on the receipt and shows it to the keeper. It never pretends to enforce what it can only record. Evidence: SHORTLIST answers, record V6, not for you V4, combined V1. Could steer: the apprentice (Q), the model gateway, the constitution (R).

## Copies know their source (`copies-know-their-source`)
Every quote, count and share keeps a link to the value it came from. Keep-until, consent withdrawal and corrections then reach every copy, a count outlives the values it was made from, and a typed copy, with no link, is shown as the weak point it is. Evidence: custody V4, V5 and V6, record V3, combined V1. Could steer: the groundskeeper (K), the edition (S), Data Atlas Triage.

## The policy is data (`the-policy-is-data`)
Every marking word, descriptor, use, keep rule, clause and phrase on a label comes from the deployment's own policy, a phrase list that is worded and translated once. A second deployment's scheme re-words every screen with no code change (S on every page here). Evidence: grammar V3 and V6, every page's S key. Could steer: the constitution (R), Admin, many deployments in general.

Also advanced: data wears its label (now explored), mine versus ours (acts named, looks counted; now explored), hear the reach (the reach ruler: limits drawn before you choose), six ways to be empty ("not told" as a fixed line on every list), reach before act (a verdict per recipient), visible repair (custody breaks that stay broken, and seals), pencil and ink (suggested labels, and the model gateway refusing labelled values), frame for urgency (the letterbox at the organisation's edge).
