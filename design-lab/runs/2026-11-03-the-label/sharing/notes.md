# Surface 4 · Sharing across floors

**The moment:** Leah Morgan shares her briefing "Fatigue and groundings, 2026" (B-31, five paragraphs). ¶3 is a count made from the pilots' duty hours (OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE once counted); ¶4, "One of them was the Marlow Star.", names which grounding and so which pilot. Four targets, all from data: the Safety committee (a named group on the Org floor, 9 people), the Marine Operations library (a whole floor, 86), All-staff news (640) and the Maritime and Coastguard Agency (outside, needs the harbour master). Every verdict comes from one function (`shareCheck`) applied to the paragraphs' labels: OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE goes to named groups, never a whole floor; PERSONAL-derived values may not leave; anything leaving needs the "ask" owner.

**Keys:** R, T, S as everywhere; each page lists its own.

---

## V1: Checkpoints
*Idea.* An architectural section through the floors, with "outside the organisation" above the Org. The paper's five paragraphs rise towards the chosen target as blocks; at the gate each one passes, passes as a count, waits for someone or stops, and the right-hand column says why. What arrives is summed up with its marking and reach.

*Sources.* Architectural section drawing; customs checkpoints; the briefing run's section (action V1).

*What works.* "What the label stops" is literally visible: the ¶4 block stays on the Department floor while the rest rise. One picture answers which floor, how many people and what gets through.

*What fails or risks.* Groups don't fit a floor section (the Safety committee is a 9-person group drawn as the whole Org level). Five blocks fit; a 40-paragraph paper doesn't. The rise is decoration once learned.

*Principles.* Explain, never hide: strong. Calm: the paper rises once per choice. Light language: the lit target level.

*Needs.* A paragraph-level label check on the server, the same one Artifacts uses at send time.

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 4

## V2: Handling slip
*Idea.* A staff distribution slip: one line per recipient with what for, until when, how many people and the label's verdict for that line. A stopped line offers the version that can go ("Send it without them"), and taking the fix keeps the reason on the slip, so the receipt says what was left out and why. Send stays disabled while any line is stopped and unfixed.

*Sources.* Staff paper distribution lists; GSC handling instructions; the briefing run's routing slip.

*What works.* Several recipients at once, each judged separately, is how sharing really happens. "A stopped line is never sent in part without you choosing it" is the right rule: no silent partial sends.

*What fails or risks.* A form; it reads as admin. The verdict text is long when several paragraphs stop. "Until" defaults (31 Mar 2027) need a policy.

*Principles.* Explain, never hide: strong. Calm: yes. Reach before act: yes.

*Needs.* Share lines with purpose and end date; partial send with a recorded reason.

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 3 · Feasibility 5 · Fit 3

## V3: Radial with the verdict
*Idea.* The cinematic run's radial menu, on a paper in the library: the Share wedge opens an outer arc of targets, each with its reach and a small verdict ("4 of 5 ¶", "3 of 5 ¶"), and the consequence readout says what arrives and what stays behind before release. The library racks back (blur and desaturate, no black scrim).

*Sources.* Cinematic run's radial menu and consequence preview (combined V2); focal planes.

*What works.* Fits the menu Harry has already accepted; the verdict at target size is enough to pick the right one without opening anything.

*What fails or risks.* Five-word reasons don't fit the readout; long clauses spill. The arc with four targets is near its limit; groups would need "Choose…". The radial is for quick acts and a stopped paragraph needs a decision, which wants the slip.

*Principles.* Focus stacking: yes. Explain, never hide: partly (reasons only in the readout). Calm: yes.

*Needs.* The radial's target list must carry a verdict per target (a shell-kit change).

*Scores.* Clarity 3 · Distinctiveness 3 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 4

## V4: Reach ruler
*Idea.* Reach as a ruler: audience size on one scale with the floors as coloured bands, the targets as ticks, and **each paragraph's limit drawn on the ruler before you choose** ("¶3 stops" between the committee and the whole department). The knob moves to the chosen target; the cards below strike what doesn't reach that far and say how far each one may go.

*Sources.* Hear the reach (room tone run) as a picture; slide rules; Isotype's single scale.

*What works.* The only variant that shows the limit **before** the choice, so you choose a target that fits rather than discovering a stop. "Goes as far as Safety committee" is a new and useful sentence.

*What fails or risks.* A log scale is honest but unfamiliar; 9 people sit in the Team band because the ruler counts people, not floors. Groups and floors on one line mix two ideas.

*Principles.* Explain, never hide: strong. Reach before act: the best. Calm: yes.

*Needs.* The check run against every target in advance (cheap: a handful of targets).

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 5 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 4

## V5: The share receipt
*Idea.* Every share leaves three records: the sender's **receipt** (what, with whom, for what, until when, under which label, what was left out and why, how to withdraw), the readers' **notice** (with "forwarding it is a new share, with its own receipt and its own check"), and an **upstream** note to the keeper of values inside ("your values went into ¶3, as a count, none named, to the Safety committee").

*Sources.* Kantara Initiative consent receipts; delivery notes; chain of custody.

*What works.* Upstream notice is new and right: Kofi learns where the pilots' hours went without asking. Withdraw says what it can and can't do ("what they already read can't be unread; anything they quoted keeps a seam").

*What fails or risks.* Three documents per share is a lot of paper; the notice and upstream note should be quiet entries in custody, not inbox items. Upstream notes for widely used values would be noise; they need a threshold (only O-S and above, say).

*Principles.* Explain, never hide: strong. Calm: depends on how notices are delivered (room tone run's answer, not announce).

*Needs.* Receipts as records; custody events across apps; "told upstream" as a policy setting.

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 4

## V6: The edge in the letterbox
*Idea.* Only one share is important enough for the letterbox: leaving the organisation. The far plane racks back; the 2.39:1 frame (from `--letterbox-ratio`, bars from `--scrim`) holds what leaves (¶3 and ¶4 struck by their labels), what it means (can't be recalled, reach unknown, marked in words), the lawful basis that must be recorded, and who must agree (Ruth Okoye; "not needed: Anita Shah, nothing PERSONAL leaves").

*Sources.* PDR-009 letterbox; rack focus (cinematic run); GSC handling outside the organisation; UK GDPR lawful basis.

*What works.* The letterbox finally has a governance use that earns it: an irreversible act with consequences outside the system. "Not needed: Anita Shah" is the explain-never-hide test for approvals.

*What fails or risks.* If outside shares are routine for a team (a port talks to the coastguard every week), the letterbox becomes a nag. It must be reserved for the first time per recipient, or for O-S.

*Principles.* Letterbox: yes, and it fits PDR-009 (nothing outside the frame hidden for good; Step back). Focus stacking: yes. Calm: one frame per act.

*Needs.* Lawful basis on outside shares; approval routing by descriptor owner.

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 4 · Fit 5

## Ranking
1. **V2 Handling slip**: per-recipient verdicts and no silent partial sends.
2. **V4 Reach ruler**: limits drawn before you choose.
3. **V6 The edge in the letterbox**: for leaving the organisation only.
4. **V5 The share receipt**: receipts, and the upstream note as a custody entry.
5. **V1 Checkpoints**.
6. **V3 Radial with the verdict**: keep the per-target verdict, add it to the existing radial.

**What I'd build first.** The slip (V2) with the ruler above it (V4), each line checked by the same server-side rule that Artifacts enforces at send. Every send leaves a receipt (V5). Leaving the organisation opens the letterbox (V6).
