A design lab run of 3 November 2026, from batch 6, "thinking made visible": **the climb from data to information to context**, and how AUGUR could make context something you can see, check, hand over and keep. Context here means the working set a person or an agent needs for one task, assembled from what AUGUR holds, with each piece saying why it is there. Sources: Ackoff's DIKW and its critics (Frické, Weinberger), Bateson's "difference which makes a difference", Endsley's situation awareness, Klein, Moon and Hoffman's data/frame theory and Weick's sensemaking, Tilden's interpretation, the nut graf, the common operational picture, and practices from outside software: court bundles, dispatch releases, impact-based weather warnings, the rules of evidence, bank reconciliation, statements of agreed facts, errata slips, breaker panels and military relief in place.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (the ladder, one reading at three rungs, the working set, three readers, two frames, stale context) and four combined screens. All sample data is one district council's renewal decision. **WS-31, "Renew the Brightwell collection contract?"**, is assembled by Tom Reyes, a contracts officer, for Grace Obi, Head of Environment and Streets, who decides by 1 Dec. Tom moves to Housing on 14 Nov, and Leah Morgan, who wasn't there, takes the set over. Contract reader, an agent under Tom, reads the contract. Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays and **T** shows the admin words under the plain ones. Start with [The life of a working set](combined/v1-the-life-of-a-working-set.html) and [What the agent was given](combined/v4-what-the-agent-was-given.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`.

Every page was checked by screenshot at 1600×900 and 1280×800 with a script that also reports console errors and sideways scroll, and a sample in reduced motion. Pages are built by `_src/build.py` from the shared `_src/common.css`, `_src/data.js` and `_src/lib.js` and each surface's `_src/v*.src.html`. The built pages link the kit as the brief says.
