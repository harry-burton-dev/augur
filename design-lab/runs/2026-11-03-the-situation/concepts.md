# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. New ones are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Holds while (`holds-while`)
Every piece of context names the condition it holds under ("holds while the offer of 20 Oct stands"). Staleness is a broken condition, not age. AUGUR watches the conditions it can, names the ones it can't, and trips a piece once when its condition breaks; only a person resets it, by reading it again. Reopening re-checks only what moved and makes a new version. Evidence: stale V2, V1 and V4, working set V2, combined V1. Sources: breaker panels, conditions precedent, dispatch release validity. Could steer: the groundskeeper (K), the edition (S), watches in Explorer, Upgrade Centre prerequisites.

## Context names its reader (`context-names-its-reader`)
Data and information belong to nobody in particular; context always names its reader, its task and its date. Per-reader context is a signed slot on a shared template whose digits are checked to be the same for everyone. A reader whose actions it wouldn't change isn't told: for them it is filed as information, quietly. Evidence: readers V3, V5 and V1, rungs V2 (the nut graf), ladder V4, combined V3. Sources: Tilden, the nut graf, Bateson, value of information. Could steer: Home "what needs you", notifications (O), the edition (S).

## Every lift is signed (`every-lift-is-signed`)
The climb from data to context isn't automatic. Each step names what lifted it. A lift by rule (comparison, threshold, rate, definition) is signed by its template. A lift by judgement (question, intent, reader, what else is going on, what happens next) is signed by a person. An agent may lift by rule and propose a judgement, but never sign one. Evidence: ladder V5, V1 and V2, rungs V1 and V5. Sources: Ackoff, Frické, Weinberger, chemical reaction notation. Could steer: the argument (Z), the apprentice's ceiling, Explorer.

## Frames, not averages (`frames-not-averages`)
When two people read the same data differently, both frames are kept side by side, each with its author, its cues, what it predicts, and the result that would change its author's mind, written before any test. They are never averaged. A test agreed in advance settles them, each rule applies itself, and the frame is rewritten with the old reading kept. The decider reads agreed facts and issues in dispute. Evidence: frames V1, V6, V5 and V3, combined V3. Sources: Klein, Moon and Hoffman's data/frame theory, Weick, double crux, pre-registration, statements of agreed facts, bank reconciliation. Could steer: the analyst's bench (AA), the argument (Z), Mandate discussion.

## What only they know (`what-only-they-know`)
Staff rotate, so anything that lives only in someone's head is an outage with a date on it. Every handover asks the person leaving, "did anything happen that isn't in the set?" The answer is drawn as a gap with a name until it is written down, and kept as said, not checked. A claim whose only witness has moved post gets its own state: its one source has moved on. Evidence: stale V6 and V1, combined V1 (stop 2). Sources: military relief in place and continuity books. Could steer: Mandate ownership changes, Admin leavers, paper and the desk (handover), the switchboard (O).

## The bundle is its world (`the-bundle-is-its-world`)
An agent's input is a working set: each piece with a direction on what it may be used for, plus a list of what it wasn't given and why. Everything it says back must cite a given piece and place. A clause that cites anything outside the bundle, or nothing, is held for a person as "a guess or a leak". A person reviews what the agent was given, not only what it said. Evidence: working set V6, combined V4. Sources: jury bundles and judicial directions; the apprentice run. Could steer: the apprentice follow-ups (ADR 0014), the model gateway, keys and doors (N).
