The second design lab run (2 November 2026): how AUGUR can show **time and history as a material**, so documents, facts, intents and releases carry visible traces of how they got here, instead of keeping history on a separate log screen. Sources: Hill and Hollan's edit and read wear, Viégas and Wattenberg's History Flow, Xanadu's parallel documents, Bret Victor's ladder of abstraction, geology and tree rings, conservators' visible repairs, tide tables and Open MCT.

Every page is a standalone prototype built only from AUGUR's tokens and Lucide icons. Each plays its moment on load, and **R** replays it. Round 1 tried six ideas on each of six surfaces after a grammar sheet named the marks. Round 2 combines the strongest into four screens that could ship. Each surface has a written critique, including what fails. Nothing here touches the product. The prompt is in `prompt.md`, and the new concepts are in `concepts.md`.
