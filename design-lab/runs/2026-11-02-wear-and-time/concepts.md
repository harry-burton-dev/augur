# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## One now (`one-now`)

One time scale and one now line shared by every time display in AUGUR, drawn once by the view rather than by each row, so ages, deadlines and distance to travel compare across apps at a glance.

Evidence: grammar V2, intents V6, combined V4.

Could steer: A shell 'Ahead' screen; a run on scheduling and capacity.

## Visible repair (`visible-repair`)

A correction is a different kind of change from an edit: it stays on the object as a seam with who, when and why, on documents, facts and deadline moves alike. From conservators' visible repairs and kintsugi.

Evidence: reader V3, intents V2, provenance V5, combined V2.

Could steer: Approvals and audit; the paper-and-desk wildcard (ledgers never erase).

## Time is matter, light is need (`time-is-matter`)

History is drawn in ink, hatch and texture and never glows; light is kept for what needs you, and that light always sits above the past. So history can be everywhere and the screen stays calm.

Evidence: reader notes, combined V1 and V4.

Could steer: Sound (B): the past is silent too. Type (C): history in weight, never colour.

## The past under water (`past-under-water`)

Everything older than now sits behind one shared translucent plane, the same water as AUGUR's shell, so 'past is quieter' is one rule, not dimming per component.

Evidence: intents V2, combined V4.

Could steer: Cartography (D): sea level and soundings; focal planes as time.

## The record has edges (`the-record-has-edges`)

The record declares its own gaps, retention limits and late arrivals. 'Silent source' (value held but stale) and 'no record' (a declared gap, drawn once across every app) are different marks.

Evidence: scrubber notes, upgrades notes, combined V3.

Could steer: Data Atlas and RAG trust; anything that shows provenance.

## Mine versus ours (`mine-versus-ours`)

Your own trace (what you read, when you last looked) is exact and private; everyone else's is a count shown only above a minimum group size. A privacy rule that becomes visible grammar.

Evidence: library V6, reader V6, combined V1.

Could steer: Presence and collaboration; notifications.

## Time as an address (`time-as-address`)

Every view can be opened at (view, time), and moving through time works like floor travel on one shared rail: a second axis of navigation, not a feature of one app.

Evidence: scrubber V1, V2, V5.

Could steer: Shell navigation; links that point at a moment.

## The overview rung (`overview-rung`)

Every large scope gets a one-cell-per-object picture you step down from, cheap and honest at any size (Bret Victor's ladder, Tufte's micro and macro).

Evidence: library V4, provenance V2.

Could steer: Cartography (D); Admin at org scale.

## Changes before they bite (`changes-before-they-bite`)

Anything about to change under you (a release's setting defaults, a migration, a deadline) is declared on the object before the moment arrives. It extends consequence preview from your own actions to other people's.

Evidence: upgrades V6, combined V4.

Could steer: Upgrade Centre; Mandate; approvals.

## Attention as wear (`attention-as-wear`)

Show how well a value has been looked after (checked, evidenced, reviewed in each period), not only what it says, so neglect is visible before it is wrong.

Evidence: provenance V4 and V5, library V1.

Could steer: Data Atlas quality; review cycles in Artifacts.
