## V1: From the move to the next player (the end-to-end flow)
*What it is.* The prompt's required flow, in seven scenes on a broken rail (May to December 2026, eleven months, November to December 2027): the move (notice to Kestrel, recorded as one-way with its dependency and clock); game over (reversed after 175 days, review started automatically for this kind of decision); replay (as it stood, own moves first; move 6 with `□`, `?` and `∞`; the two voices); filed (agreed in the room, filed under C3.2 with motifs and two lines of theory); a year on (Leah drafts D-163, in book until her step 3, the margin speaks); her move (she plays R-07 move 6 first, privately, then asks Dana); the meeting (D-163 goes with two seams and a line). The left pane is the game's life; the right pane its score sheet. The moment lands on "a year on".

*Built from.* Replay V2 and V4, marking V2 and V4, voices V1 and V5, collection V1, learning V1, V2, V5 and V6.

*What works.* It shows that one record does the whole job: nothing is copied; the book, the margin and the next decision all point at the same review. The first scene shows what AUGUR must record during work for any of this to be possible. Dana's answer ("I did what the decision said; the order was nobody's choice. Make it yours.") is the no-blame review working as intended.

*What fails or risks.* Seven scenes is a story, not a screen; it is for explaining the idea to Harry and to a floor, not a product page. Scene 1's "the records add one line on the day" is a proposal (it depends on recording dependencies).

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 5 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 5

## V2: The review board
*What it is.* The review mode of a Mandate decision. Left, the score sheet with the people's marks, the records' sign and the split icon; moves still to come out of focus by default (F shows them). Middle, the position as it stood: the records' word with the `∞` chip, the three lines with headroom, the last three things on the record, and hindsight behind H. Right, the marking panel: the mover's own mark (released), the reviewer's, the `∞`, the six keys, and the four parts of a line (known then, chosen, instead on the record, with the idea). Below, the curve, stopping at the selected date, with records-found moments (circles) and people-found moments (squares).

*Built from.* Replay V1 and V2, voices V1 and V5, marking V1, V2 and V6, grammar V5.

*What works.* Everything a reviewer needs on one screen, and nothing that would let hindsight in by accident. The curve stopping at the date is a small, strong touch. The two kinds of moment markers show the run's finding at a glance: people found the moments before the records did.

*What fails or risks.* Three dense panes; on 1280 it is at its limit. The after-action review (marking V3) should be the screen's first tab, not missing; this board is the second step.

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 4 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 5

## V3: The book
*What it is.* Find by position at the top (a sentence of four facets fills in as the moment), landing on the C3.2 page: its book line and the deviation, the explorer at the step that decides it (2 games each way, "Four games. Not a rate: read them."), the games in this position including a transposition (R-04, a phone line moved out to a contractor, same position) and a locked one with whom to ask. On the right: motifs in these games, theory, and reach.

*Built from.* Collection V1, V2, V4, V5 and V6; voices V6 (theory).

*What works.* Search by where you stand, read by code, learn by motif, with the base on every count. It is the opening book the prompt asked for, and it doesn't need anyone to browse a taxonomy.

*What fails or risks.* The explorer bars still invite causal reading. Needs an owner for codes, motifs and theory per deployment.

*Scores.* Clarity 4 · Distinctiveness 5 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 4

## V4: In book, while you draft
*What it is.* Mandate's drafting screen for D-163. Her steps on the left, each "in book" with the R-07 move it matches. The margin is silent until step 3, then speaks once: R-07 turned at this step; play that moment first (the puzzle inline, her choice marked "yours", the marks); then what R-07 asks of her draft (three questions with their moves). The moment changes her step 3 to the book line, with a seam, and adds a line.

*Built from.* Learning V1, V2, V5 and V6; collection V1.

*What works.* The whole learning surface in one calm margin: silence, one note, a choice, three questions, a seam. It is the screen where the review pays back, inside the task, which is the batch's requirement ("no time for a method unless it pays back inside the task").

*What fails or risks.* Depends on recognising the position (facets at draft time) and matching steps; both need work in Mandate. If several precedents match, the margin needs a rule for which one speaks (the closest, then "2 more games like this").

*Scores.* Clarity 5 · Distinctiveness 5 · Feasibility 3 · Fit 5
