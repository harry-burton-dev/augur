A design lab run of 3 November 2026, prompt AA from batch 6, "thinking made visible": the **method** half of reasoning. How AUGUR could put the right analytic technique at the point of work for people who have never been trained as analysts: the person describes the question, not the technique; it runs on their own Data Atlas facts and Mandate intents; it says how long it takes before it starts; and its result stays beside the output it shaped, to be scored later. Sources: Heuer's *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* and analysis of competing hypotheses, the US Government's *Tradecraft Primer* (key assumptions check, quality of information check, devil's advocacy), Klein's premortem, Kahneman and Lovallo's outside view, Tetlock and Gardner's keeping score, Snowden's Cynefin, Dewar's assumption-based planning, Ishikawa, the WHO surgical safety checklist and the nominal group technique.

The run has 36 prototypes over six surfaces (the bench, choosing a technique, competing explanations, checking assumptions, the premortem, the working kept) and four combined screens. All sample data is one organisation (Vale Regional Services) and the contract renewal the briefing and minute runs also used: Facilities deciding whether to renew Calder Waste on the North round (intent INT-208, decided as D-142 on 10 Dec 2026, read again a year later). Confidence words come from the briefing run's yardstick, worked out by the same rule; decisions carry the minute run's status beside the words and its revisit lines.

Each page plays its moment about 1.2 s after load. **R** replays, **T** shows each technique's trade name and source one step down, and **G** turns the model off (its pencil disappears; no mark, count, word or result changes). Start with [The renewal, end to end](combined/v1-the-renewal.html) (← → through the five stages) and [From working to watches](combined/v3-working-to-watches.html). The prompt is in `prompt.md`, the ranking in `SHORTLIST.md` and the new concepts in `concepts.md`.

Every page was checked by screenshot at 1600×900 and 1280×800 with a script that also reports console errors and sideways scroll; the combined screens were also shot at each stage. Pages are built by `_src/build.py` from the shared `_src/common.css`, `_src/data.js` and `_src/lib.js` and each surface's `_src/v*.src.html`; the built pages link the kit as the brief says.
