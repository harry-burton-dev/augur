# Prompt AA: The analyst's bench. Thinking tools for people who aren't analysts.

Read `design-lab/brief.md` first; it holds the rules, kit, critique format
and run shape. When you start, move this file to
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-analysts-bench/prompt.md`.

---

Batch 6 is about **thinking made visible**. This prompt is about method:
the analytical techniques, mental models and frameworks that make
judgement better, applied by ordinary people to their own work.

Intelligence analysts, forecasters and engineers use **structured
techniques** to catch their own biases: list every hypothesis before
choosing one, check the assumptions underneath a judgement, imagine the
plan has already failed, take the outside view. These techniques work,
but they live in training courses and on whiteboards, and almost nobody
uses them on an everyday output. This run asks how AUGUR could put the
right technique **at the point of work**: the person writing a briefing,
planning against an intent or choosing between suppliers gets the one
technique that fits the question, run on their own facts, with the
result kept beside the output. Work in
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-analysts-bench/`.

## Direction
- **The technique fits the question.** Diagnosis, choice, forecast,
  plan and explanation each get different tools; a person describes the
  question, not the technique's name.
- **Run on real facts.** Techniques take Data Atlas facts and Mandate
  intents as their inputs, not blank templates.
- **The working stays with the output.** An assumptions check or a
  premortem is attached to the briefing or decision it shaped, so a
  reader can see how the judgement was tested.
- **Cheap to start, quick to finish.** Most techniques take minutes;
  each says how long it will take before it starts.
- **A model is a sparring partner, in pencil**: it may play devil's
  advocate or propose a hypothesis nobody listed; it never scores the
  matrix.
- Explain, never hide; many deployments; calm at rest (from the brief).

## References and what to take from each
- **Richards Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999)** and
  **Analysis of Competing Hypotheses**. Take: a matrix of hypotheses
  against evidence, judged by what is inconsistent, not by what agrees.
- **The US Government's *Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic
  Techniques* (2009)**: key assumptions check, quality of information
  check, indicators, devil's advocacy, team A/team B, high-impact
  low-probability, what if. Take: a shelf of named, short techniques.
- **Gary Klein's premortem (2007)**. Take: "it is a year from now and
  this failed; why?", done before committing.
- **Kahneman and Lovallo's outside view (1993)** and reference class
  forecasting; **Tetlock and Gardner, *Superforecasting* (2015)**. Take:
  start from how things like this usually go, then adjust; keep score.
- **Dave Snowden's Cynefin framework** (clear, complicated, complex,
  chaotic). Take: choose the method by the kind of situation.
- **Mental models** (Munger's latticework, inversion, second-order
  effects, Fermi estimation) and **root-cause tools** (Ishikawa, five
  whys). Take: small named tools a person can learn in one use.

## Surfaces (6 variants each)
1. **The bench** (grammar): kinds of question, the technique for each,
   time to run, what it produces, where the result is kept.
2. **Choosing a technique**: a person describes what they are trying to
   decide and is offered one technique, with why.
3. **Competing explanations**: ACH on a real question, built from Data
   Atlas facts, with the most inconsistent evidence lit.
4. **Checking assumptions**: the assumptions under a Mandate plan, each
   marked solid, supported with caveats, or unsupported, with an
   indicator that would show it failing.
5. **The premortem**: a team imagines an intent failed; the reasons
   become risks and watches.
6. **The working, kept**: a finished briefing or decision showing which
   techniques shaped it, for a reader a year later.

Round 2: combine into 3-4 screens. One must follow a Department deciding
whether to renew a contract: outside view, competing explanations for a
fall in performance, an assumptions check, a premortem, then the
decision with its working attached.

## Questions the shortlist must answer
- Which six techniques ship first, and which are left to training.
- Whether the bench belongs in Explorer, Mandate, a kit part any app can
  open, or the shell.
- How results feed watches (indicators) and confidence is worked out,
  and what the events service would need.
