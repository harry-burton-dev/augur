## Critique
*Idea.* A structure change is shown three times, to the three people it
touches, in the order they meet it:
- the admin sees the **cost** before paying it;
- the keepers get the cost as **small, site-shaped questions**;
- the operator meets the change **once, on the record**, and keeps a quiet
  trace of it.

The kind's fill bar ties the three together: the admin's "702 gaps" visibly
closes as keepers answer.

*Sources.* Darkroom proof prints and the cinematic consequence preview;
planning-consultation notices (who is told); postal rounds and field survey
cards; StreetComplete and Wikidata's missing-statement quests; library
reclassification slips and errata slips; conservators' visible repair (the
seam).

*What works.*
- "One question per site per keeper" is the strongest new idea here. 702
  gaps become 31 questions, each answerable in a minute with the evidence in
  front of the person. Rounds are a person-shaped view of Triage, not a new
  queue: a "yes" accepts a document's proposal.
- The preview's "who will be asked" block is exactly the set of rounds that
  saving creates, so the admin sees the work they are causing.
- "Can't tell" keeps the gap honest. The fill bar never closes on a guess,
  and a passed-on gap names its next person.
- The operator's record proves the six marks hold together on one card
  without noise: each has a different shape (corner ticks, the word None,
  muted words, hatch on the age, a wavy break, a 135° hatch with a lock).
- The slip appears once and becomes a seam. The record keeps its shape
  history, and calm returns.

*What fails or risks.*
- Rounds need a jobs model Data Atlas lacks: assignment per keeper per site,
  reassignment, expiry, "Later". A large change can still mean 20 questions
  for one keeper, so it needs a cap and a later pile.
- The fill bar's "keeper" segment uses the corner-tick gap mark stretched
  over a long span. It reads as an empty bracket, which is honest but faint;
  a faint hairline fill was added under it.
- The paper slip is still the one light surface in a dark shell (toned from
  92% to 84% ink). Several unread slips on one record need a rule: newest
  on top, "and 2 earlier changes".
- Per-person read state for structure changes, and "last visit", don't exist
  today.
- At 1280×800 the consequence list scrolls. "Make the change" stays reachable
  in a sticky footer.

*Principles.* Explain, never hide: "what won't change" is explicit, hidden
rounds and records are drawn locked with whom to ask and "some" for counts,
and every closed gap names its person and source. Focus stacking: the slip
racks the side column back (opacity and saturation, no blur on the record).
Calm: the preview develops once, three arrivals then still, the slip folds
once. Letterbox: not used, since a schema change is important but not
urgent. **Pushback:** the Mandate intent has a deadline (1 December). If it
slips, the urgent path, not this screen, should carry it.

*Needs (tokens).* Shared with V3: `--x-proposed-gap*`, `stale`, `unseen`,
`wait`, `far`, `type-micro`. Only here:
- `--x-proposed-new` / `new-wash`: the structure changed here;
- `--x-proposed-seam`: the quiet dashed seam;
- `--x-proposed-paper` / `paper-ink`: the slip surface.

Literal values left: 1–2px hairlines and segment radii, the `blur(2px)` of
the developing preview, the `.55` dim of the draft bar and `.75` slip
metadata. The kit should name a "draft" opacity. It relies on `--font-mono`
(ADR still owed).

*Needs (kit).* `FillBar` (four segments plus a hidden stub), `Blank` (six
kinds), `NoticeSlip` → `Seam`, `RoundCard` with the typed blank (`Comb`).

*New employee test.* **Pass** for keeper and operator: one site, one
question, three buttons; a note on the record that folds away. **Pass** for
the admin preview.

## What Data Atlas would need (against docs/atlas.md and its API)
Today it has `POST /ontology/changes` (one new version), `GET
/ontology/versions` (who, why, changes), proposals and observations with
sources, and Mandate bindings per entity. To serve this screen it would also
need:
1. **A preview endpoint** for a draft change, counted from today's records:
   - records by floor (and whether a floor's count may be shown off-floor);
   - the expected-detail gap breakdown;
   - documents that could answer, via Artifacts;
   - keepers per site.
2. **Expected details per kind** with a fill count per detail per floor,
   which also feeds the fill bar.
3. **Keepers:** a steward per site, or per kind per floor, so a gap has a
   person. This gives the "nobody named" segment its meaning.
4. **Rounds:** proposals grouped by keeper × site, with assignment,
   reassign ("Not mine"), abstain ("Can't tell", passed to the lead) and
   progress. A round's "yes" is the existing accept-observation step,
   batched.
5. **Proposals from documents for a new detail:** reading candidate values
   from Artifacts documents into Triage at save time.
6. **Bindings per kind and per detail** from Mandate and Explorer (today
   per entity), so "which apps' views change" can be counted.
7. **The change log joined to records**, per-viewer read state and last
   visit for the slip and seam, and the change's reason and asking intent
   stored on the version (versions already carry "why").
8. **Stated absence** (None), **applicability** (doesn't apply), **review
   age** per detail (stale) and **declared record gaps** (no record) as
   distinct values, so the six marks come from data, not layout.
9. Counts for floors the viewer can't see follow the safe default "some".
   **Exact counts are a decision for Harry.**

## Where it goes beyond the ask
- One question per **site** per keeper, not per record.
- "Also worth a look": the same kind of date on Vehicles and Sites.
- A fixed-width hidden stub on the operator's bar, so a proportion can't
  leak a count.
- "Can't tell" passed to the steward, not left in the round.
