# Concepts found by this run

Each is bigger than one prototype and could steer a future run. They are also filed in `design-lab/concepts/`.

## Six ways to be empty (`six-ways-to-be-empty`)
An empty-looking slot always says which kind of empty it is: not known yet (with who could fill it), none, doesn't apply, stale, no record, not yours to see. One mark and one sentence each, the same in every app. Grows out of the honest blank.

Evidence: grammar V6, card V2 and V5, facets V5, change notes, combined V2 and V4. Could steer: the kit, Artifacts metadata, the label (L), the groundskeeper (K).

## Said in a sentence (`said-in-a-sentence`)
Kinds, filters and chains of links all read back as plain sentences that can be shared, attached to an intent, or used to place something new.

Evidence: placing V6, facets V3, shape notes, combined V1-V3. Could steer: the reference desk (I), the briefing (M), the index (P).

## The structure predicts its gaps (`the-structure-predicts-its-gaps`)
Lay the structure out so its holes show; what people tried to put there gathers on the empty slot with a count, and admins grow the structure there.

Evidence: placing V5, shape V2 and V4, grammar V2, combined V3. Could steer: ontology admin, Explorer, the constitution (R).

## Waiting is a place (`waiting-is-a-place`)
A thing that can't be placed yet has its own visible state that names who decides and keeps everything said.

Evidence: placing V2 and V6, combined V3. Could steer: Triage, Mandate asks, the commission (V).

## Change arrives as work (`change-arrives-as-work`)
A structure change turns its gaps into named rounds of small jobs, previewed before saving, then a notice once and a seam.

Evidence: change V4, V6, V1, combined V4. Could steer: the groundskeeper (K), Upgrade Centre.

## Two registers (`two-registers`)
Plain word on top, the admin word under it on the same rows; anyone may turn it on, only admins change it; studio words stay in the studio.

Evidence: grammar V6, card V6, every surface's T key. Could steer: Admin, Explorer, keys and doors (N).

Also advanced: plain words first and hierarchy without containers (now explored), consequence preview (facet counts, structure change) and visible repair (notice then seam).
