# Prompt U: The case file. Bundling work from many places into one output.

Read `design-lab/brief.md` first; it holds the rules, kit, critique format
and run shape. When you start, move this file to
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-case-file/prompt.md`.

---

Batch 5 is about **work outputs**: what operators make with AUGUR and
hand to someone else (a published document, a decision, a case bundle, a
request fulfilled, a filled-in form). AUGUR's pillar apps hold the pieces
(Artifacts, Mandate, Data Atlas); these prompts ask how a finished piece of
work is made, checked, delivered, and trusted later, across apps.

Real deliverables are often bundles: a case file, an incident report, a
due-diligence pack, an evidence bundle for a hearing. The pieces come from
several apps (documents from Artifacts, facts from Data Atlas, the intent
from Mandate, people, decisions). This run asks how an operator
**assembles a bundle** that is complete, labelled, consistent and
traceable, then hands it over and keeps it current. Lawyers, police and
clinicians build bundles like this under strict rules. Work in
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-case-file/`.

## Direction
- **A bundle has a table of contents with page numbers,** and every item
  points to where it came from.
- **Completeness is checked:** what a bundle of this kind must contain,
  and what is missing (honest blank).
- **The strictest label wins, and says so** (data wears its label).
- **Live or frozen:** a bundle is either kept current or frozen at a
  date, and says which.
- Many deployments; calm at rest (from the brief).

## References and what to take from each
- **Court bundles** (UK practice directions: paginated, indexed,
  agreed). Take: a strict, shared shape.
- **Police case files and evidence lists.** Take: exhibits with
  references and continuity.
- **Clinical records and discharge summaries.** Take: a summary on top of
  a full record.
- **Due-diligence data rooms.** Take: an index, access per party, and
  questions asked against items.
- **Journalists' dossiers and fact-checking.** Take: every claim with its
  source.

## Surfaces (6 variants each)
1. **Bundle sheet** (grammar): bundle, index, exhibit, tab, missing,
   frozen, live, label.
2. **Assembling**: gathering items from across apps into a bundle.
3. **The index**: the bundle's contents, with sources and gaps.
4. **Completeness**: a checklist for this kind of bundle.
5. **Handing over**: sending the bundle with its label and reach.
6. **Keeping it current**: what changed in sources since it was made.

Round 2: combine into 3-4 screens. One must build one incident report from
five sources, freeze it, then show what changed a month later.

## Questions the shortlist must answer
- Is a bundle a document in Artifacts, a new record type, or a view?
- How references to items in other apps survive changes and withdrawals.
- What completeness rules look like per deployment.
