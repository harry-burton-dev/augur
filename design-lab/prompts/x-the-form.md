# Prompt X: The form. Templates and repeatable outputs.

Read `design-lab/brief.md` first; it holds the rules, kit, critique format
and run shape. When you start, move this file to
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-form/prompt.md`.

---

Batch 5 is about **work outputs**: what operators make with AUGUR and
hand to someone else (a published document, a decision, a case bundle, a
request fulfilled, a filled-in form). AUGUR's pillar apps hold the pieces
(Artifacts, Mandate, Data Atlas); these prompts ask how a finished piece of
work is made, checked, delivered, and trusted later, across apps.

Much work is the same shape again and again: a monthly report, an
inspection, an onboarding pack, a risk assessment. Organisations keep
templates in shared drives and they drift. This run asks how **repeatable
outputs** work in AUGUR: a template with its own version and owner, a
filled-in output that remembers which template made it, fields that fill
from Data Atlas, and templates that improve from how people actually use
them. Government form designers and checklist makers know how to make
forms people finish. Work in `design-lab/runs/<date>-the-form/`.

## Direction
- **A template is an output too,** with an owner, a version and users.
- **The filled output remembers its template** and version, and says
  when the template has moved on.
- **Fields fill from what we know,** and say where each value came from.
- **One thing per page:** forms that ask one question at a time when
  that helps.
- Explain, never hide; many deployments; calm at rest (from the brief).

## References and what to take from each
- **GOV.UK Design System patterns** ("one thing per page", question
  pages, check answers). Take: forms people finish.
- **Atul Gawande's *The Checklist Manifesto*** and aviation checklists.
  Take: short, tested lists with pause points.
- **Pattern books and builders' plans** (Georgian pattern books). Take:
  a template that leaves room for local variation.
- **Mail merge and document assembly** (legal document automation).
  Take: a template filled from records.
- **Caroline Jarrett's *Forms that Work*.** Take: the conversation a form
  has with a person.

## Surfaces (6 variants each)
1. **Form sheet** (grammar): template, field, filled, source, version,
   check answers, pause point.
2. **Making a template**: fields, sources and the owner.
3. **Filling it in**: one thing per page, values filled from Data Atlas.
4. **Check answers and submit**: the summary before it becomes an output.
5. **When the template changes**: outputs made from older versions.
6. **How it is used**: where people stop, skip or change a template.

Round 2: combine into 3-4 screens. One must follow a monthly report template
through three months, one template change, and its outputs.

## Questions the shortlist must answer

- What "fill from Data Atlas" needs from its API.
- How this agrees with mise en place (J) and the edition (S).
