# Batch 3: data for operators

Written 3 November 2026 at Harry's request: prompts on data structure,
asking data questions, transforming it, looking after it, governing it
and presenting it, for operators who are neither technical nor data
savvy. The batch was written while seven prompts were still waiting, so
the queue now holds fourteen; run order is Harry's call.

Who it is for: an operator on a Department or Team floor who has never
written a query, never seen a schema, and wants answers they can trust
and act on. Every prompt tests its ideas against that person.

Five new concepts came out of the research, all **to explore**:
`plain-words-first`, `the-workings-show`, `the-recipe-is-the-record`,
`care-is-scheduled` and `data-wears-its-label`.

Suggested order if Harry wants one: H first (everything else rests on
people understanding what is kept), then I and M together (asking and
presenting are two halves of using data), then J, L, K. Batch 1 D (data as
landscape) overlaps M and H; run it after H.
