# Prompt V: The commission. Asking for work and accepting it.

Read `design-lab/brief.md` first; it holds the rules, kit, critique format
and run shape. When you start, move this file to
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-commission/prompt.md`.

---

Batch 5 is about **work outputs**: what operators make with AUGUR and
hand to someone else (a published document, a decision, a case bundle, a
request fulfilled, a filled-in form). AUGUR's pillar apps hold the pieces
(Artifacts, Mandate, Data Atlas); these prompts ask how a finished piece of
work is made, checked, delivered, and trusted later, across apps.

Mandate holds intents: what the organisation has asked for. This run looks
at both ends of an ask as outputs: the **brief** someone writes, and the
**delivery** someone accepts. What makes a good ask (outcome, deadline,
what "done" looks like), how the person doing the work agrees to it, how
changes to the ask are handled, and how the asker signs it off. Architects,
commissioning editors and procurement teams do this every day. Work in
`design-lab/runs/<date>-the-commission/`.

## Direction
- **An ask says what done looks like,** in plain words, before work starts.
- **Agreement is explicit:** the doer accepts, questions or declines.
- **Changes to the ask are visible and agreed** (visible repair; changes
  before they bite).
- **Sign-off is a moment:** delivered, checked against the brief,
  accepted or sent back with reasons.
- Explain, never hide; many deployments; calm at rest (from the brief).

## References and what to take from each
- **The architect's brief and the RIBA Plan of Work** (stages with
  sign-off). Take: stages and gates for an ask.
- **Commissioning editors' briefs** (word count, angle, deadline, fee).
  Take: a short, complete ask.
- **Purchase orders and goods received notes.** Take: matching what was
  asked against what arrived.
- **Acceptance criteria and definition of done** (agile practice). Take:
  checkable conditions.
- **Change orders in construction.** Take: a change to the ask as its own
  agreed record.

## Surfaces (6 variants each)
1. **Commission sheet** (grammar): brief, accept, query, decline, change
   order, deliver, sign off, send back.
2. **Writing the ask**: a brief with what done looks like.
3. **Taking it on**: the doer's view of a new ask.
4. **Changing the ask**: a change order, agreed or not.
5. **Delivering and signing off**: checking delivery against the brief.
6. **The asker's book**: everything someone has commissioned, and its state.

Round 2: combine into 3-4 screens. One must follow one ask from brief, one
change order, delivery, a send-back, and final sign-off, from both sides.

## Questions the shortlist must answer
- What Mandate intents hold today and what a brief and sign-off would add.
- How deliverables (documents, bundles, facts) attach to an ask.
- How this agrees with the score (F) and the minute (T).
