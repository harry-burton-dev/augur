# Batch 6: thinking made visible

Written 3 November 2026 at Harry's request: research directions in art
style and design, in applying analytical, mental-model, framework and
logical reasoning techniques to people's knowledge work, and in the climb
from data to information to context, with intent applied to context.

The batch reads as one climb. The house style (Y) sets how all of it looks.
The situation (AB) lifts data to information to context. The argument (Z)
and the analyst's bench (AA) are how people reason over that context, and
the intent (AC) says what to do with it. The wildcard (W6) reviews the
result afterwards.

Who it is for: the same operator as batch 3, on a Team or Department floor,
who has never been trained as an analyst and has no time for a method
unless it pays back inside the task.

Letters ran out at Z, so prompts after Z take two letters (AA, AB, AC).

Four new concepts came out of the research, all **to explore**:
`the-warrant-shows`, `the-technique-fits-the-question`,
`context-is-a-working-set` and `intent-travels-two-levels`.

Suggested order if Harry wants one: AB first (the argument, the bench and
the intent all take context as their input), then AC, then Z and AA
together (structure and method are two halves of reasoning), and Y
whenever: it touches every screen and doesn't depend on the others. Run
W6 after the minute (T), which it reviews.

Overlaps to watch: Z with the briefing (M, run) and the minute (T); AA
with confidence is worked out; AB with the briefing's heights and the
apprentice (Q); AC with the commission (V) and keys and doors (N); Y with
the cinematic run and type as instrument (C).
