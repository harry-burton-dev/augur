# Answer, don't announce

AUGUR stays silent and only things that wait for you answer: on arrival, when you ask, or when something you are waiting on changes. Sound marks a change of state, never the state itself, and background work is heard only on demand.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-room-tone`](../runs/2026-11-03-room-tone/) (floors V6, ambient V4 and V6, combined V1 and V3).

Could steer: Notifications, presence, Home.

Explored in [`2026-11-03-the-switchboard`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-switchboard/): information never pulses and waits for the edition; that kept 15 deliveries in the sample week from interrupting.
