# The warrant shows

Every claim in an output shows what supports it and why that support counts (Toulmin's warrant), not only a citation. A claim is no stronger than its weakest step, and the page names that step. Objections attach to the step they attack and stay until answered. From Toulmin, Minto's pyramid, Wigmore charts and argument mapping.
