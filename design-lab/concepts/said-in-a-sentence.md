# Said in a sentence

Every structure an operator meets reads back as a plain sentence: a kind is "a ___ that ___", a filter is "contracts about waste in the North that end this year", a chain of links is "an invoice is against a contract, which is with a supplier". The sentence can be shared, attached to a Mandate intent, or used to place something new.
