# The technique fits the question

People describe what they are trying to decide (explain, choose, forecast, plan) and AUGUR offers the one analytic technique that fits, run on their own facts, with its time to run stated first. The result stays attached to the output it shaped, so a later reader sees how the judgement was tested. From structured analytic techniques (Heuer's ACH, the key assumptions check), Klein's premortem, the outside view and Cynefin.

Proposed by batch 6 research.

Could steer: the analyst's bench (AA), Explorer, Mandate planning.
