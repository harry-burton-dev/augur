# Judged as it stood

A choice is judged by what was known when it was made, not by how it turned out. A replay shows each step as it stood: what was on the record, what was still unknown, the records' view as at that date; steps still to come sit out of focus in the far plane, and hindsight comes on its own wash, behind a key, after the mark. A `?` needs an alternative that was on the record at the time; without one it is `?!` at most. From outcome-bias research (Baron and Hershey 1988) and "resulting" (Annie Duke).
