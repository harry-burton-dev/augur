# The bundle is its world

An agent's input is a working set: each piece with a direction on what it may be used for, and a list of what it wasn't given and why. Everything it says back must cite a given piece and place. A clause that cites anything outside the bundle, or nothing, is held for a person as "a guess or a leak". A person reviews what the agent was given, not only what it said. From jury bundles and judicial directions on the use of evidence; it extends the apprentice run's agent rules.
