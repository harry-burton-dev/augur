# Six ways to be empty

An empty-looking slot always says which kind of empty it is, with one mark and one sentence each, the same in every app: not known yet (a gap, with who could fill it), none (someone checked), doesn't apply, stale, no record (time the system lost) and not yours to see (whom to ask). A gap can close; no record can't. Grows out of the honest blank.
