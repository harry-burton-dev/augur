# The next step is shown

Every item that waits on someone says who will be asked next and when ("If nobody takes it, Dana Whitfield is asked at Fri 08:30"), worked out from a policy the whole team can read. Escalation stops being a surprise and becomes a schedule: on the card, on a team board, and at the head of the morning edition.
