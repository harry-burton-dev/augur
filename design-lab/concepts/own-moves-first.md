# Own moves first

The person who made a move marks it before anyone else; the reviewer's mark stays sealed (shown as a sealed card saying who and when it opens) until they release theirs. Then both stand, signed, side by side; the mover may reply but marks don't change. The meeting agrees the review as a record, not each mark, and nothing anywhere counts anyone's marks. From self-annotated games, the after-action review (those who were there speak first) and blind second marking.
