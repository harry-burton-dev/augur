# Status beside the words

A record's words are agreed once and never edited; its status (in force, worth revisiting, amended, reversed, complete, lapsed) is computed from dates, watches and later records, and every citation reads it live at word size. A correction is a seam; a change of mind is a new record that points back.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-minute`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-minute/) (grammar V1 and V4, record V1 and V5, combined V2).

Could steer: The edition (S), the case file (U), the form (X), any record other apps cite.

Related: [status is a mark](status-is-a-mark.md) (paper and the desk) agrees that status never changes by itself; here every change is a dated act (made, line crossed, kept, reversed) and the status is read from them. [Visible repair](visible-repair.md) for corrections.
