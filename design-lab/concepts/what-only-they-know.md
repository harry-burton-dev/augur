# What only they know

Staff rotate, so anything that lives only in someone's head is an outage with a date on it. Every handover asks the person leaving, "did anything happen that isn't in the set?" The answer is drawn as a gap with a name until it is written down, then kept as said, not checked. A claim whose only witness has moved post gets its own state: its one source has moved on. From military relief in place and continuity books.
