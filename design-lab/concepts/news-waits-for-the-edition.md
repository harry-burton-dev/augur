# News waits for the edition

Only what needs someone interrupts. Everything else (info, and whatever came in quiet hours) waits for a predictable edition at the start of the person's day, in a fixed order: waiting on you, overnight, for the record, nothing from. Every source is entered even at zero, so silence is a checked blank, and urgent is the only stop press. The emailed copy is a pointer, never a copy.
