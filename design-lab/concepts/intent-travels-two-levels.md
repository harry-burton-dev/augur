# Intent travels two levels

Every intent says its purpose, end state and limits, and leaves the method open. Anyone can see the intent of their own floor and the floor above, and how their part serves both. What reaches a person is ranked by the intents it touches, a change made within intent needs no approval but records how it still serves it, and understanding is confirmed by a backbrief rather than assumed. From mission command, Bungay's *The Art of Action*, Klein's intent statements and Hoshin Kanri.
