# Every if has a then

No condition without its action: "nothing to do on most servers" names the servers that must act and what they do. Writing a text as an if-then table is also the fastest way to find what it never says, which then becomes `[open: ...]` rather than silence. From the Federal Plain Language Guidelines (tables for conditions) and decision tables.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-safety-card`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-safety-card/) (one-note V4, next-note V3). Not tested on its own.

Could steer: settings help, error messages, policy text, ops runbooks.
