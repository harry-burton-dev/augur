# A contradiction is open

A rewrite that keeps every fact keeps every contradiction. Where a source disagrees with itself, the rewrite marks it `[open: ...]` and names who can settle it, instead of carrying both sides into a cleaner text that looks surer.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-safety-card`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-safety-card/), by the readers rather than the author: AUGUR's upgrade guide says apps take "only 0.x patches" in one note and "patch and minor releases" in another, and uses "by hand" for two different things. The rewrites kept both.

Could steer: any run that rewrites or merges existing text, Data Atlas's disputed facts, Mandate intents that conflict.
