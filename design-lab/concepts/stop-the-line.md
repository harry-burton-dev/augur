# Stop the line

Anyone who can see an agent’s work can pause one kind of it at once, with their name and a reason. Nothing is undone and nothing is lost; only the supervisor restarts, narrows or keeps it stopped, and the person who pulled it is told what was decided. The smallest safe action belongs to everyone affected, the decision to the person accountable. From Toyota’s andon cord and jidoka.
