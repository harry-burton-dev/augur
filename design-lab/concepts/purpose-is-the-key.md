# Purpose is the key

Nobody asks for "access". They ask to use something for a purpose, until a date, and the purpose is checked against the label as they choose it. The grant, the share and the consent all carry it, and each leaves a receipt both sides keep and either can withdraw.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-label`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-label/) (not for you V1, V4 and V6, sharing V2 and V5, custody V4, combined V2). From Kantara consent receipts and GSC need-to-know.

Could steer: Keys and doors (N), approvals, the switchboard (O).

In [`2026-11-03-the-intent`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-intent/): a derived grant follows the intent's freedom and the backbrief, and ends at the review date, a change to that clause, or when the person leaves (within V6).
