# Works under someone

Every agent has a named supervisor and a deputy, an errand anyone affected can read in a sentence, a scope (where it may read, propose and act, floor by floor) and limits (model calls a day, a schedule). These show wherever its work appears, not only in Admin. The supervisor answers for its work, as a nurse who delegates stays accountable; the agent always signs as itself, never as a person. No supervisor, no run: if the post is empty, the agent pauses.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-apprentice`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-apprentice/) (grammar V1 and V5, scope V6, combined V4).

Could steer: Keys and doors (N), the switchboard (O), any service that acts for a person.

In [`2026-11-03-the-situation`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-situation/): an agent's working set shows its supervisor, its mark and its mode line (combined V4).
