# People mark moves, records mark positions

A review has two voices answering different questions. The records say where a decision stood on any date, worked out by rule from the lines it set itself (weakest line wins, never typed); people say whether each choice was sound with what was known then, in a small fixed notation (`!! ! !? ?! ? ??`), signed. Never averaged or scored together; a split between them is a finding, filed under one of four reasons (the records can't see it, hindsight, the line was set wrong, two people differ). A person's only mark on a position is `∞`, which lowers certainty and never moves the number. From Chess Informant symbols and engine evaluation bars.
