# Every lift is signed

The climb from data to information to context isn't automatic. Each step names what lifted it. A lift by rule (a comparison, a threshold, a rate, a definition) is signed by its template. A lift by judgement (a question, an intent, a reader, what else is going on, what happens next) is signed by a person. An agent may lift by rule and propose a judgement, but never sign one. The rung is shown as a three-step stair and a word, never a colour. From Ackoff and his critics (Frické, Weinberger) and chemical reaction notation.
