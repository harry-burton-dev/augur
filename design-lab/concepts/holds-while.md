# Holds while

Every piece of context names the condition it holds under ("holds while the offer of 20 Oct stands"). Staleness is a broken condition, not age: a three-week-old count that still holds stays quiet, and a new piece whose condition broke is flagged. AUGUR watches the conditions it can (a document withdrawn, a fact revised, a person moving post, a date passing), names the ones it can't, and trips a piece once when its condition breaks; only a person resets it, by reading it again. Reopening re-checks only what moved and makes a new version, and a decision names the version it rests on. From breaker panels, conditions precedent and dispatch release validity periods.
