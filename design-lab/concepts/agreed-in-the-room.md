# Agreed in the room

Minutes are agreed as they are made: the line is read back and each person present answers "that is what we decided". Assent to the record is kept apart from agreement with the decision, so someone who abstained still confirms the minute is right. The model may hear and draft in pencil; only the room inks.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-minute`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-minute/) (live V4 and V2, combined V3).

Could steer: Approvals, the commission (V) sign-off, any record made by several people at once.

Related: [the readback moves it](the-readback-moves-it.md) (paper and the desk): the same read-back, applied to a handover rather than a minute.
