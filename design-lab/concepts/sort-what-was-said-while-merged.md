# Sort what was said while merged

Undoing a merge is easy; undoing what people said while it stood is the work. Every fact, link and binding made against the merged entity must be sorted to one part, one row each, with who and why, and the fact stays one row (no copy). Until it is sorted it is shown as waiting, never silently left on the senior. The same holds for other apps' links to the merged entity, which learn of the split through events.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-type-specimen`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-type-specimen/) (merge V2 and V4, identity V4's failure, combined probes). The delivery note's address, said in April, was the subsidiary's.

Could steer: Mandate (intents bound to a merged entity), Artifacts (documents tagged while merged), a sorting queue in Data Atlas Triage.
