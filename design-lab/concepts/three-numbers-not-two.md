# A rerun has three numbers, not two

A saved answer rerun later gives three numbers: as asked, as it stood then knowing what we know now, and now. The step from the first to the second is what we learned about the past; the step from the second to the third is what happened. A fourth frame (as asked, with today's access) isolates "different access". So each of the five reasons is worked out by which clock moved, not guessed. Showing only "then and now" hides which changes were corrections.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-type-specimen`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-type-specimen/) (across time T3 and T4, every Q2, combined): 7, 9, 8.

Could steer: the briefing (watch for change), Upgrade Centre release notes ("what changed in what you already had"), the switchboard (which notice is a correction).
