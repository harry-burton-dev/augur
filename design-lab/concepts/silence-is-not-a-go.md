# Silence is not a go

An agent does every reversible part of a job itself and holds at the first step it can’t take back. A standby has five states: standby, standing by (acknowledged, not a yes), go, hold and lapsed. It carries a go-by time and asks a deputy by rule; asking another person is the only thing its clock does by itself. A standby nobody answers lapses, not done: the system fails safe, like a signal returning to danger. Sheridan and Verplank’s veto timer (“goes ahead unless you stop it”) is never used.
