# Two drifts

Work drifts from an intent (worked out from records: the share of hours on a key task, a rate against the line) and an intent drifts from the world (an assumption it rests on stops holding). The first moves the needle, the second moves the line; the first is a question about the work, the second about the intent. Each is asked once of its owner, at the crossing, in a muted warm tone, never in the Letterbox, and "amend the intent" is always one of the answers.
