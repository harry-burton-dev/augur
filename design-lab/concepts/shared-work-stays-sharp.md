# Shared work stays sharp

Ink (initials, outline, position) says who holds a thing and its state; light says only how urgent; depth may lift one person's own focus but never blurs work another person holds, because peripheral sight of a neighbour's work is how a handover is seen coming. Argues against focal planes on shared views.
