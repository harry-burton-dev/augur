# Frame for urgency

The 2.39:1 letterbox for urgent and important things (PDR-009). The bars carry information, and nothing outside the frame is hidden for good.

First found in: [`2026-11-02-cinematic`](../runs/2026-11-02-cinematic/).

Could steer: Type (C): the urgent sentence as a headline inside the frame.

Used in [`2026-11-03-the-apprentice`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-apprentice/): a standby whose go-by falls before the supervisor's next look opens the Letterbox, by the shell's rule, never the agent's choice (standby V2, combined V3).

Used in [`2026-11-03-the-switchboard`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-switchboard/): urgent at night in the Letterbox, with the bars carrying the escalation schedule (escalation V6).
