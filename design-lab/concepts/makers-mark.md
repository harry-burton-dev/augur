# The maker’s mark

Work made by an agent carries its monogram (two or three letters set by its app). The mark is dotted while the work is pencil and solid once a person accepts it, and it stays on the work for good beside “accepted by …”, as an assay office stamps its mark beside the maker’s. It tells agent work from people’s at text size, in any app, without a robot icon, and lets a reader find everything one agent made.
