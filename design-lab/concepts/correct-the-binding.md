# Correct the binding, not the order

What reaches a person is ordered by a fixed rule from the intent parts each item is bound to (a limit and your own part first; untouched folded, never hidden), and every item can show the trace: tag, record, part, rule. If the order is wrong, a binding is wrong; you correct the binding (for everyone it reaches), never drag an item up or down. A model may only suggest a binding for an untagged item, in pencil.
