# Mistakes have siblings

An agent’s errors are systematic, so one found means more were made the same way. A flag recalls the lot (same agent, kind, source, layout, runs) for checking together; each repair leaves a seam with the whole chain named; and the review asks why the checks missed it (a random sample can miss a systematic error; two readers with the same wrong idea agree) before what to change. From product recalls by batch and airworthiness directives.
