# One sheet for the jump

A reader crossing many changes at once needs their actions merged by when they act (one table of settings, one list of steps not to skip, one table of what going back costs), not one note after another. The sheet is generated from the notes for the range being crossed, never written by hand, because by hand its upkeep is the worst of any form. From the safety card's order and run sheets.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-safety-card`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-safety-card/) (whole-set V3, combined Part A). Reader test: the only text faster than the source (678 words per answer against 896), at 28% of its length, with the fewest doubts.
