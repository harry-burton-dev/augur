# Told, not asked

A change inside an intent's freedom, by someone who has said that intent back, needs nobody's yes. It becomes a notice to the floor above that says how it still serves the intent (built from what was checked, never typed), with "Seen" and "Ask a question"; a question undoes nothing. Anything outside freedom still asks the named holder of that choice, and anything across a limit asks the limit's owner. Approvals shrink to the decisions that really belong above.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-intent`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-intent/) (within V6 and V2, combined V1 and V4; mission command's disciplined initiative ("act, then report"), schemes of delegation).
