# Context names its reader

Data and information belong to nobody in particular; context always names its reader, its task and its date. Per-reader context is a signed slot on a shared template whose digits are checked to be the same for everyone, so nobody's reading changes what was counted. A reader whose actions it wouldn't change isn't told: for them it is filed as information, quietly. From Tilden's interpretation, the nut graf, Bateson's difference that makes a difference and the value of information.
