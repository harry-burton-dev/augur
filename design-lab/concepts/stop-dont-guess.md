# Stop, don't guess

A step that can't read a value stops at the first misfit of its kind, asks with the row's own context (its note, its neighbours, who else could answer) and offers the other answers; nothing after the stop runs and nothing half-done lands. It stops only when a guess would change what lands, groups misfits with one cause into one stop, and is the only thing on the bench that uses light. From Toyota's jidoka, applied to data rather than to agents (compare stop the line).
