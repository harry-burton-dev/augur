# Play it first

A precedent reaches the next person inside their task, at the step where it turned: silent while their draft follows a reviewed game, it speaks once at the matching step. Before reading what happened they play that moment as it stood and choose, privately; then they see what was played and the marks. The lesson becomes a few questions on their draft, each citing its move and keepable with a reason; changes carry a seam naming the game. From de Groot's choice-of-move studies, "guess the move" training and decision-forcing cases.
