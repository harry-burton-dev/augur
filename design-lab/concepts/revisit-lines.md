# Revisit lines

Every decision states at the table what would reopen it (a line on a record it rests on, a date, an assumption) and AUGUR watches them. Crossing one marks the decision "worth revisiting" and asks the chair once; a watch never reverses anything. A decision with no line stays in force until someone remembers it.
