# Pause points

Each kind of decision has a few fixed moments where a technique is offered (when the question is set, when a plan is on paper, before signing), as a surgical checklist has sign in, time out and sign out. At each, AUGUR reads what the records already say and asks only the question that changes the offer; between them, nothing is offered. A skipped pause point says so, with a reason, rather than vanishing. From the WHO surgical safety checklist and Klein's premortem as a step before committing.
