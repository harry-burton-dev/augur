# Confidence is worked out

Confidence words come from a fixed yardstick, worked out from source grades and agreement, never typed; a person may only lower one. Counts take no word, they say what share of the period their records cover. A dispute is two answers lifted off the scale and a gap is an empty mark, neither gets a word. A summary is no surer than its weakest claim.
