# Rehearse on last week

Every setting is previewed by running your own recent history through it again: "with these hours, last week 3 more things would have waited for your start"; "if Leila had taken it, 2 people would have been asked, not 4". A consequence preview made from real data, not an example, so a person sets quiet hours, mutes or a rota against what actually arrives.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-switchboard`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-switchboard/) (why V3, switchboard V2 and V3, owner V6, combined V2).

Could steer: the constitution (R) for policy changes; the meter (W4); feature flags and rollout settings.
