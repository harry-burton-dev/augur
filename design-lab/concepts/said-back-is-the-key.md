# Said back is the key

An intent passed down is an open question to each holder until they say it back in their own words and its owner confirms or corrects it. Freedom within the intent unlocks only for confirmed holders: a new arrival on rotation, or a team that hasn't said it back, still asks. The words show what was understood, and what wasn't said is shown to the owner before confirming.
