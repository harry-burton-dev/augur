# The empty knows its errand

Each way to be empty sends its own errand: not known yet becomes a Triage job for the keeper, not yours to see becomes a share request, "no place for it" becomes a Mandate ask, and no record says plainly that nobody can close it. The errands are previewed before they are sent.
