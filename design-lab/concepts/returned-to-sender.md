# Returned to sender

Nothing an app sends fails silently. Every notice that reached nobody (not allowed on the floor, team notices off, already told) comes back to its sender with the reason, how many people it would have reached, and the one person who can change it, with an Ask button. A dry run finds the same answer before the send.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-switchboard`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-switchboard/) (owner V2, V5 and V6, combined V4).

Could steer: Admin, Apps; the groundskeeper (K) for upkeep alerts; keys and doors (N) for permission requests.
