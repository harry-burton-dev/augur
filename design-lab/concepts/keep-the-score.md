# Keep the score

Every tested judgement is scored when the future arrives: each assumption held, partly held, failed or can't tell; each premortem reason happened or didn't; each forecast came true or not. The score is kept for floors and techniques, never for people. A technique must also change a line of the output to count as paid, and one that changes nothing in most runs leaves the shelf for that floor and says why. Over years, a floor can see whether its "about half" means half. From Tetlock and Gardner's keeping score and the after-action review.
