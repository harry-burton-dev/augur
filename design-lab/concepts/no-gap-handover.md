# No gap between owners

Responsibility passes only when the next person takes it, as in an air traffic sector handoff. Offering an item, escalating it or naming a cover never moves ownership by itself; the owner keeps it until someone says "I have it", and everyone else is then stood down by name. Passing it on carries a handover note, half written from the trail, so the next person never starts from nothing. A cover has to say yes before away begins.
