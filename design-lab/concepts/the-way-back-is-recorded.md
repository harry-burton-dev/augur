# The way back is recorded

A step that can't be undone is marked one-way when it is taken, with its way back and what that costs, what it depends on and the clock it runs against; options on the table and assumptions (with a date to test them) are recorded too. Then the records can say on the day "a one-way step was taken before what it depends on", instead of a review finding it months later. From the engine horizon, chess's only move and time trouble, and critical path.
