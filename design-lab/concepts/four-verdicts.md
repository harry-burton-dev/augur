# Four verdicts

Every option set against an intent gets one of four verdicts, by rule from the record: within; within but against a key task (allowed, with the cost said in the intent's own units); ask a named person (outside freedom); across a limit (only its owner can lift it). Nothing is filtered out of sight: the limit bin is shown with its owner. "Within, against a task" is the honest middle that approval schemes lack.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-intent`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-intent/) (grammar V6, within V1 and V5, combined V1 and V2; Bratman's intention as a filter on options).
