# Upgrade while someone is awake

Schedule change for when the watch can judge it and a person can act on it. At night the watch is too quiet to judge errors and the one admin is asleep; a restore that would cost nothing at 02:00 waits until it costs the morning. The cheapest 2am card is the one the window makes unnecessary. Pushes back on ADR 0020's default (Sunday 02:00), so it needs Harry's decision.
