# Verdict first

Every piece of text that may ask something of its reader opens with one of a small, closed set of verdicts (for upgrade notes: do before, by hand, check if, nothing to do) and what undoing it costs, before any why. A reader who stops after one line still does the right thing. From the inverted pyramid, GOV.UK front-loading and the safety card's fixed panels.

First found in: [`2026-11-05-the-safety-card`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-safety-card/) (one-note V1 and V2, whole-set V4, combined template). Reader test: the card per note built on it was as accurate as the source and no faster; useful as data for a generated sheet, not shown to help reading on its own.

Could steer: Home "what needs you", the Upgrade Centre's Needs you list, notifications, ops runbooks.
