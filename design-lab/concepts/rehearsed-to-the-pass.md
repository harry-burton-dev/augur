# Rehearsed to the pass

Automation may prepare everything but never sends. A saved recipe runs when its input arrives, up to the check before anything leaves, and waits there; lines that run as before stay silent, only a stop reaches "Needs you", and a person always does the pass. A normal month makes no noise at all. Prep is automated; judgement is scheduled, not skipped.
