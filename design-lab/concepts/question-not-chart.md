# Question, not chart

Operators pick the question they want answered (is it getting worse? which is biggest? where?), never a chart type. Each question has one picture and one sentence template, and the chart's title is the finding. A never list (pie, 3D, two scales, gauges, scaled symbols, bars not from zero, stacked areas, shaded maps of raw counts) ships as data with its reasons. The lead's follow-up questions are also how people move between heights.
