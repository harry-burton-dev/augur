# Trust changes the sample, not the signature

A person signs every fact an agent proposes; trust only changes how much of the work they look at first. At the batch rung a run’s work waits as one; AUGUR draws a sample from a fixed table, across sources and their layouts; one wrong sends the batch back, and the sample is recorded on every detail it let through. A detail read by only one reader is always sampled; a disagreement never goes in a batch. From acceptance sampling and dual keying.
