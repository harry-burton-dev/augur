# What doesn't fit decides

An explanation is judged only by the facts against it, never by how many fit. Facts that fit every explanation are shown grey, because they can't tell explanations apart; an explanation nothing has tested can't win; two explanations left standing is a result, and the page names the one fact that would split them. A fact that is only "possible" counts half and can't rule anything out alone. From Heuer's analysis of competing hypotheses.
