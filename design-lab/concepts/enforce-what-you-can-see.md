# Enforce what you can see

AUGUR enforces the parts of a label it can watch happen: who opens a value, where it is shared, whether it reaches the model gateway, whether it leaves the organisation, how long it is kept. For purposes it can't observe (appraisal, say), it asks, records the declared purpose on the receipt and shows it to the keeper. It never pretends to enforce what it can only record.
