# The question is the record

A saved question keeps its words, each sharpening choice (and who made it), its steps, when it was asked and whose access each run used, but never the rows. Reruns never call the model. Share the question, not the answer: each reader reruns with their own access, and a fixed copy may only go where every reader can open every source behind it.
