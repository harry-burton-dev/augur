# Quiet has named keys

Quiet hours are trustworthy because the exceptions are listed and owned: each route that can get through (urgent on your on-call days, the next step of an urgent nobody took, email for urgent, people you let through) says who cut that key and when it was last used. Everything else waits, and the page counts what waited, so the cost of quiet is visible too.
