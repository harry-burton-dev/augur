# Copies know their source

Every quote, count and share keeps a link to the value it came from. Keep-until, consent withdrawal and corrections then reach every copy, a count outlives the values it was made from, and a typed copy, with no link, is shown as the weak point it is.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-label`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-label/) (custody V4, V5 and V6, record V3, combined V1).

Could steer: The groundskeeper (K), the edition (S), Data Atlas Triage.

In [`2026-11-03-the-situation`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-situation/): working-set pieces are links, never copies.

In [`2026-11-05-the-type-specimen`](../runs/2026-11-05-the-type-specimen/) (data track): a `quotes` row records where a run's count was copied (a briefing), so a correction can reach it.
