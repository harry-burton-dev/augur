# The wake

A decision has a wake (the actions it set, the documents that cite it, the forecasts that count it, the later decisions that lean on it) and AUGUR can know it because each holds a reference. Revisiting shows the wake before the vote; reversing turns it into seams and named jobs and tells exactly the owners it touches, nobody else.

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-minute`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-minute/) (revisit V2, V3 and V6, record V3, combined V1 and V2).
