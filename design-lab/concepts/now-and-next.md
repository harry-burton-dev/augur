# Now and next

Wherever automation works, one line says what it is doing now (in words: “Holding” and “Stopped” are modes, never blanks), what it will do next, what will stop it, and why it last changed. A changed cell is boxed once, for a few seconds, then the line is still again. From aviation’s flight mode annunciator and the research on automation surprises: the four questions people ask of automation answered in one place.
