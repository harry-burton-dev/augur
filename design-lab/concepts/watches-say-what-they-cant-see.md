# Watches say what they can't see

A watch lists the events that would move what it watches and marks those AUGUR can't notice yet (a due date passing makes no event today), so silence never reads as "nothing changed".

First found in: [`2026-11-03-the-briefing`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-briefing/) (action V5, combined).

Could steer: The switchboard (O), the events service, the groundskeeper (K).

In [`2026-11-03-the-switchboard`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-switchboard/), escalation needs the same events-service clock as watches.

Used in [`2026-11-03-the-minute`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-minute/): a decision's revisit watch lists what it can't see (revisit V1).

In [`2026-11-03-the-situation`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-situation/): a working set names the conditions AUGUR can't watch (stale V2).

Used in [`2026-11-03-the-analysts-bench`](../runs/2026-11-03-the-analysts-bench/): signposts and tripwires are drawn even where AUGUR can't see them; a missing clock left a crossed line unseen for 12 days (assumptions V4, premortem V6, combined V3).
